Tutorial Organization

This tutorial is organized into the following sections:

- What is a Wind Turbine?

An introduction to the components of a typical wind turbine, along with the specifics of the "Active Stall" family.

- Physical Principles

A description of the fundamentals of subsystems in a generic wind turbine and specific data related to industrial wind turbines.

- Active Stall Two-Speed Wind Turbine Simulator

The structure and components of this simulator. Since it runs on the LdP platform, it can also be used as a Test Bench where you can change subsystem parameters to observe various behaviors. You'll need to understand the LdP platform concepts covered in the "Introduction to LdP" tutorial.

- Practical Labs

Guidelines for conducting experiments that let you reproduce situations that occur in real wind turbines, both under normal and exceptional conditions. You'll observe their effect on different internal variables and study the influence of different parameters. Each lab contains step-by-step instructions.
