The Blades used in Wind Turbine are similar in concept to the propellers of airplanes. The idea is that you insert a rigid body in a flux of air to gain part of the energy in that flux, stopping it somehow. The form of that rigid body, and its position respect to the air flux and the mechanism that support that body, is such that it can stand turning around some axis and in a stable position.

In  "Basic concepts" you can find an introduction to the physical concepts involved in both taking energy from the wind and using the wind to stop the rotor.
