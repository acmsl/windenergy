A common problem in the hydraulic circuits is the loss of pressure due to leakage. There may be pressure leakage in the valves, or in the pump, or in the pipes, or in the tank.

One way to simulate this situation is to modify the value of the "Parameter.NacelleBrake.LeakageBrakeHydrCircuit".  As a previous test, you may change its default value (0.01) to a higher value (ie, 0.5) and observe the evolution of several signals:

•The "Hydraulic pressure" widget you will observe that it descends much faster, and also that the "Pump" is active more often.

•If you select the "Synoptic"->"StripRecorders"->"Hydraylic circuit: Panel to monitor watching operations in the hydraulic circuit of brakes", you will observe a plot as in next figure.

In the first recorder corresponding to "Nacelle.Braking.BrakeCircuitPressure", there are two lines, one at 100 value and another just below it: the distance between these two lines corresponds to the hysteresis value of the pressure switch.

You can observe that this increment in leakage forces a start of the Pump more often.

With leakage in the range of "2" the plot change as shown in next figure.

In short time the "Opened incidences" tab will register the message 17 ("Pump starts too often").

If we "repair" the problem, restoring the leakage to a "normal" value (ie, 0.02) the evolution of these signal will change. See next figure.

As soon as the Controller can "measure" the time needed to start again the pump, the register in "Opened incidences" will be annotated with the "recovered" instant (see next figure). 

If you "acknowledge" the incidence the Controller will try to cutin again, automatically.
