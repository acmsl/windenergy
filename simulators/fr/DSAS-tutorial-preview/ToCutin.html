Repeat the sequence described in the section "Setting the conditions for a Wind Turbine to start operation" bringing up an StripRecorder with the panel "State Machines + Pitch + RPMs: Panel to monitor the evolution of internal State Machines + Pitch + RPMs".


The evolution of the Controller's state machine is a good way to understand how the evolution of the Cutin process is done. The next figure shows the evolution of both state machines (Operation and STOPsequences) as well as the evolution of the RPMs of the Generator. 

Upon reception of the START command, the Operation SM receives the order (event) for untwisting power cables, followed by a check that there is enough wind for going to cutin in Large mode. The Controller increases the pitch angle to take energy from the wind although the rotor is stopped (brakes are on). This process permits to check that the brakes are working properly before starting a cutin process. After the adequate time out the Operation SM receives the "brake released" event, moving to the  "SG2Cutin" state (Going to Cutin), till it receives the "ECrosCutin" event (RPMs crossed the Cutin speed), at which moment the Controller fires the Thyristors during some seconds, monitoring the current in the generator (to trip down the process in case of any problem). The current flowing from the Grid to the generator in this state is used to magnetize the generator. After the conduction angle for the Thyristors reach its maximum value, the Bypass contactor is tuned On and the thyristors are no longer fired.


The Operation SM arrives to the "SGG" state: production with Large connection.
