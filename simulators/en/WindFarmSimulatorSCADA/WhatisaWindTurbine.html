What is an aerogenerator?

Introduction ››

An aerogenerator is a power production plant from kinetic wind energy. It can be described with a set formed by akinetic wind energy converter to rotating kinetic energy, which is attached to arotary kinetic energy to electric energy converterI mean an electric generator.

There are several types of wind turbines, with a first distinction concerning thearrangement of shovels: of vertical or horizontal axis. Virtually all wind turbines in Wind Parks are horizontal.

They can also be distinguished according to thetype of electric generatorandhow it is connected to the Electric Network. It also differs in the method of control of the power obtained from the wind: whether it is by control of the attack angle of the shovels (variable or not).

El tipo mas común de aerogenerador industrial, de los que se pueden encontrar en parques eólicos, es el de palas rígidamente unidas y girando en un plano vertical que es mantenido frente al viento; por tanto el eje de giro se mantiene. Se conocen con el nombre de HAWT (HorizontalAxisWindTurban areas).

The wind-to-rotatitva energy converter is composed of the shovels, which are subject to a "hub," which is in turn attached to an axis that transmits the mechanical power to a multiplier. The output of the multiplier which is connected to the electric generator.

The set formed by the shovels, the hub, and the rotor that connects it to the multiplier is known as "Power Train" ( DriveTrainin English). The Power Train, the(gearbox)in English), thebrake system,guidance mechanism (yawin English) are mounted on a platform that can rotate horizontally on the top of the Tower, and is known asGondola (Nacellein English). The power generation system (electric generator y power electronicswhere appropriate) are also located within the Gondola. The power cables fall vertically through the centre of the Tower until they reach the electric cabinet located at its base, where they connect with the output cables to the Network.
