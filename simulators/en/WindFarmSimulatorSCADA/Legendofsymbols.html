Switch: can be opened or closed in any situation. The connection or disconnection can be made'in hot', which means that there will be internal electrical arches that these devices are designed to support (by a maximum number of 'operations'), without risk to the other devices nearby or to the human operator.

Sectionator: It must only be changed in state when there is no current or there can be no current through it. If it opens when there is current passing through it or closes when its edge connected to the wind turbines has load (C-1, or C-2 or C-3 closed), then an electric arc is produced. In this case, the devices usually remaindamaged.

The line that links the two switches indicates "interlocking": i.e., in this case, the sectionator that connects to the evacuation line cannot be operated if thesectionatorwhich connects the internal line to the ground (this position is used as a method of security in operations within the Substation).
