Change of the state of an aerogenerator

Practical classes ››

To perform this practice make sure that the "Automatic control"is not active.

1.Choose an aerogenerator that is in production.

2.Click on your column "Start/Stop": observe what happens to"Stop"and that the Total Power is decreed with the value of the power that this wind turbine was producing.

3.Choose an aerogenerator that is in "Stop".

4.Click on your "Start / Stop" column: see what happens to "Production"and that the Total Power is increased with the power value of this wind generator.
