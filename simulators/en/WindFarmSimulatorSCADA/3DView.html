3D view

Description of the Eolico Park Simulator ›› Simulator components ››

The simulator includes an interactive 3D view. The control of this separate synoptic is at the centre of the SCADA synoptic, labelled with "3D view of the Park"

The sign contains the name of the wind turbine, the orientation of its Gondola, and the rpms of the shovels.

You can change the view by pressing the left button while dragging the mouse.

To change the view to a given aerogenerator is selected with "double click"in the view SCADA.
