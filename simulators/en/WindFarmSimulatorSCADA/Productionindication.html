Production indication

Description of the Eolico Park Simulator ›› Simulator components ›› Main synoptic ››

On the bottom right are the indications of the "Total potential"that is delivering the park, and of the total power it could give ("Total power available"), if all wind turbines could be put into production for current environmental conditions.

The Total Power indicator has a background color that serves to indicate the ratio of the indicated value to theDemandrequested to the park.

If it's colorGreen, means that production is within thepermitted power rangewhose center is the value of the current demand and its lower and higher limits are defined by the parameter "SetPointTolerance"(seeParameters.)

If it isRedindicates that current production is above the upper limit ofpermitted power range. This situation may result in economic penalties, because it does not meet the terms of the supply contract.

If it isYellow, indicates that the current production is below the limit of the permitted power range, and it is therefore possible to start more wind turbines to increase it, if available.
