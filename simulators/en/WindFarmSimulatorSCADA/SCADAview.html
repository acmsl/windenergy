The description of each of these columns, functionalities and meanings is as follows:

•Column "ID": internal indicator of a given aerogenerator.

•Column "State"

Not available

For some reason, the wind turbine cannot / should not be launched.

Stop

The wind turbine is available, but it is not currently in production.

Production

The wind turbine is in production


•Column "Name": Identification name of the aerogenerator.

•Column "Model": type of wind turbine.

•Column "Use": if the check-box is not marked, this aerogenerator shall not be taken into account in the start-up / stop algorithm of aerogenerators. SeeAutomatic management.

•Column "Start/Stop": this box allows to start or stop the wind turbine

With double click the wind turbine passes to "Stop," available but without production.

•Column "Est. Comm": indicates the state of communications between SCADA and the wind turbine.

At that moment, SCADA is asking the wind turbine to send him his state.

SCADA has successfully received the status data requested from the wind turbine.

SCADA did not receive a correct response to its request for a state.

•Column "Pot. Nominal": the nominal power of the wind turbine. KW.

•Column "Pot. Real": the power that the wind turbine is producing at that time. KW.

•Column "Wind": the wind measured by the aerogenerator anemometer at that time. In m / s

•Column "Guidance": indicates the direction of Gondola at that time. In degrees, between 0 and 360.

•Column "Hours. Prod": indicates the number of hours of production of the wind turbine. This value is used to decide which are the wind turbines to start or stop in aautomatic park management.
