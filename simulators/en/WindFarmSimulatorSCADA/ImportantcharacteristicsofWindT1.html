En What is an aerogenerator?The industrial aerogenerator components are described. From the perspective of themanagement of the wind farmthe generic characteristics of an industrial aerogenerator are as follows:

1.An aerogenerator is aIndustrial power production plantwhich does not need any external information for its operation. I mean, they'reautonomous plants.

2.The power it delivers depends on the speed of the incident wind, but not on its direction (as they are automatically oriented). It also depends on control parameters: at a given time limits may exist for the power generated by an aerogenerator.

3.Its power cables, which ultimately communicate with the Network, are connected to the Common Connection Point (PCC), located in the electrical substation.

4.They have communication channels, usually using fiber optics, through which they are connected to theSCADAFrom the park. Through these means:

1.It can be controlled.
This control includes automatic start-up ("START") and ordered stop ("STOP"). Depending on the type of wind turbine, it is also possible to control theMaximum active powerandreactive powerto deliver to the network.

2.The wind turbine responds with information regarding its state.
The information provided typically includes:

1.Su status: in production or stop.

2.Thenetwork voltage levels.

3.La powerwhich is generating, active and reactive.

4.Wind: speed and direction.

5.La energy producedfrom a prefixed moment.

6.Temperaturesof key elements: environment, generator, multiplier, bearings, lubricating oils, etc.

7.Certain operating parameters with which it is operating: power limits, etc.

8.Incidences(alarms, messages, etc.) generated by the Controller.
