This sensor gives the wind direction. Its output may be:

(a) in the form of an analogue signal (typically a voltage).

(b) or as an output of an absolute digital encoder.

The reference of the veleta must be calibrated to the geographical North.

In the case of analogue output, it is also necessary to calibrate the output value for 360º from the reference (North).
