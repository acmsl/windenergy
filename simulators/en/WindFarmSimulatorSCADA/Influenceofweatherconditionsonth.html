Influence of environmental conditions in the Park Production

Practical classes ››

The production of an aerogenerator is proportional to theair density.

1.In the "Weather station"increase theroom temperature. Check that air density decreases when temperature increases, and vice versa.

2.Increase theambient humidity. Check that air density decreases by increasing moisture, and vice versa.

3.Increase theatmospheric pressure. Check that air density increases by increasing pressure, and vice versa.

Note how Total Production changes corresponding to changes in air density.

If the "Automatic control"is active, and the wind level is right, you can see how these changes influence the management of the park.
