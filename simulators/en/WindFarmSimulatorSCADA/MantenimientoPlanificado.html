El Corrective maintenanceIt covers all operations related to the repair of one of the elements of the Aerogenerators, or of the other elements of the Park that are broken.

The elements that have the most such incidents in the wind turbines are:

•The shovels:
They break or they break.

•The Electric Generator:
It's burning. Without using contact of the roating rings (in the DFIGs), wear problems often exist.

•The Pitch mechanism of the shovels:
Those that are based on hydraulic cylinders can be clogged and supply failure.
Those that are based on electric engines can be burned.
In both cases the electronic control elements (amplifiers, controllers, etc.) can be broken.
They can also

•The Multiplier:
It can suffer different internal failures, in its gears, generally related to mechanical sofreefforts on the low-speed axis (the one connected to the Hub and the Paddles) and also by sudden changes in the high axis (the one connected to the electric generator)

•The Rotor Cojinet:
This element is the one that supports a higher demand for mechanical effort, as it must bear the weight of Hub and Palas, and changes derived from the wind (direction and bursts)

•Brake discs:
Normal and extraordinary wear-out due to fast braking.

•Condenser banks:
Some aerogenerators (especially those with a squirrel cage generator directly connected to the Network) incorporate capacitor banks that are used to correct the power factor delivered by each aerogenerator. These banks have a certain useful life, lower in which they do not use connection by typewriters.

Some of these operations involve the use of elements outside the Park (e.g. cranes) which greatly increase them.
