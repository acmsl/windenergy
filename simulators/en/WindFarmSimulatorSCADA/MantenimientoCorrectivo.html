Preventive maintenance

Operations in a wind farm ›› Maintenance Management ››

This maintenance is more of a work philosophy that includes several tasks. It is generally supported by special instrumentation that is usually incorporated into wind turbines and allows for the collection of additional data on various elements. Similarly the multiplier, the rotor bearings and the generator.

The data collected are processed to detect signal evolution patterns that indicate changes in the internal behavior of these elements. There can be great savings in the operation of the park if a possible break-up of these elements is detected in sufficient time in advance.

This potential savings can come from several sources:

•better planning of non-existing auxiliary elements in the park, such as large cranes. For example, by grouping the work into several wind turbines to avoid the cost of moving these cranes.

•better planning of the material to have in stock. Generally, certain elements, such as multipliers, may have very long delivery times (months), which requires a forecast of breakages to store a sufficient number of them, in order not to incur serious loss of profits.

•reduction of the magnitude of the failure: an early detection of a defect, for example in a tooth of a gearbox, can be solved with technical and supply requirements (e.g., local) at a much lower cost than if the failure is not detected (and therefore not repaired), which can lead to a much greater failure, which forces the entire multiplier to change.
