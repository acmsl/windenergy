Automatic management

Description of the Eolico Park Simulator ›› Simulator components ›› Main synoptic ››

In a real wind farm there are other systems that facilitate Operation and Maintenance tasks, taking into account aspects related to Planned Maintenance, logistics of parts, equipment and consumables, personnel management, etc.

These aspects influence the management of wind turbines (start-up or step-by-step) in relation to production. For example, if an aerogenerator is to be put in place, and there are several available, one will be preferred to another according to different criteria.

In order to exemplify this influence of additional management aspects, this simulator has chosen to include a start-up or start-up management mechanism for a given aerogenerator using a criterion that has to do with:

•el number of hours in productionfor each aerogenerator (see column "Hours. Prod"inView of the SCADA application )

•the choice of the operatorto allow that aerogenerator to participate in that operation (see column "Use" inView of the SCADA application )

It is possible to control the operation of this "automatic management" mechanism of the aerogenerators through thecheckboxlocated at the centre of the synoptic and labelling "Automatic control"

The algorithm used monitors the power produced and checks that it is within the limits defined by thepermitted power range(seeProduction indication). If it is above, it calculates the minimum set of wind turbines that, if they do not produce, the total power of the Park is the maximum possible within the permitted range, and "proposes it" to the SCADA operator. See figure below.

The operator has a few seconds (see parameter "WatingTimeCancel Proposal"inParameters) to "Cancel" the proposal: otherwise, the stop-by of the indicated wind turbines is executed.

Similarly, if the power being produced is below the limit of the power range, the algorithm calculates the minimum set of wind turbines that makes the power generated up to a value below the maximum limit.
