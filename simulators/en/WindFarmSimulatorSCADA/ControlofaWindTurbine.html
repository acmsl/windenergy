Operating modes

The wind turbine has twomode of operation:

1.Manual:This mode is used for the maintenance of the wind turbine. It allows manual control of different subsystems (orientation, pitch, etc.), and access to sensor variables, electrical measures, etc. Due to the large number of options, when you enter this mode of operation you use a device with keyboard and screen (portatil computer, PDA, etc.), known as "Control Terminal".

2.Automatic:This is the normal way. In it, the aerogenerator monitors the environmental variables and performs the necessary operations to produce electricity according to them, or to remain in standby. In this way the Controller is keeping an eye on the orders you can reach through the communications channel you have with the SCADA system of the wind farm.

The functions of the Controller can be grouped according to the following criteria:

•De production control. 

There are at least two "START" and "STOP" functions, which indicate to the wind turbine controller that a process of "cutin" (network connection and production) and "cutout" (network output and disconnection) must be initiated.

Some wind turbines also allow them to be fixedMaximum active power, always for a value equal to or less than the nominal; andreactive power.

•De access to production data.

The Airpower Controller sends through the communication link the values of the important variables (tensions, currents, powers, energy produced, temperatures, etc.) as well as of the incidents (alarms, warnings, messages, etc.) that have been produced.

•De access to internal control parameters.

Generally this functionality can only be done locally, and requires restricted access administered with words of passage.

•De manual controlof certain elements.

For example, the control of the engines that allow the orientation. These functionalities can only be done locally, usually also with access by words of passage.

Overallthere is no standardization of communications protocols, or data formats, for remote access to these functionalities.
