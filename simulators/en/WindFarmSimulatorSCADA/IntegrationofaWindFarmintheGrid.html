Integration of a wind farm into the Electric Network

Introduction ›› What is a wind farm? ››

The Electric Network at the level of a large territory is organized in several layers, so that there is a set of elements that make the "transport"from production plants (thermal, hydraulic, nuclear, combined cycle, wind, photovoltaic, etc.) to the vicinity of the consumption points. The distance between the nodes of these Networks can be hundreds or even thousands of miles. This function uses very high voltage, with several ranges depending on distances and other criteria, but typically between 130KV and 400 KV, of a three-phase network. In some sections the connection between the nodes of these networks is made with continuous current, with tensions above 400 KV. This part of the Network is known as"High voltage network"or also"Transport Network"

The points of consumption can be industrial, residential or large cities. The supply to large industrial customers and large cities is made with another type of network, called "Media Network", triphasic with tensions between 12KV and 60 KV. In large urban agglomerations the supplies are made in Low Tension (less than 1kV).

The objectives of this structure are:

•Minimize transmission and distribution losses.

•Increase the control of the global system.

•To have sufficient flexibility to respond to changes in consumption.

In most countries, the management of the High Tension Network is responsible for State-controlled entities, while the Distribution Networks are controlled by independent companies.

Since each wind farm has a significant production, from the point of view of influence in the global of the Network, they are usually connected directly to the Alta Network.

The production of wind energy is already a fundamental component of the energy mix. For example, on the Spanish Electric Network (https://demanda.ree.es/generacion_acumulada.html) the participation of wind production in Spain can be observed.
