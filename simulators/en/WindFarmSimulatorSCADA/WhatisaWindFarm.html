What is a wind farm?

Introduction ››

We can describe a wind farm as a power production facility from the wind, composed of several aerogenerators and a set of auxiliary elements.

They generally export their production to the National Electricity Distribution System, although there are also particular facilities where they are supplied directly to other plants or industrial customers, large electricity consumers.

Its main elements are:

•TheAerogeneradroes

•El power cabling systemconnecting these aerogenerators to the Common Connection Point (PCC)

•La Electrical Substation, which connects the Common Connection Point (PCC) to the External Electric Network.

•El Monitoring and Control System (SCADA)where certain Aerogenerator Operation data are collected and can be controlled on March and Paro.

•El Production Measurement System, located in the Electric Substation.

•La Meteorological Station. 

•Otherelectrical safety and control systemssuch as those intended for the Control of the generated reactive power.

On many occasions, the Monitoring and Control System (SCADA) of a park is connected to another SCADA located in a remote location, to which other SCADAs from other parks are also connected, and where all of them are added.
