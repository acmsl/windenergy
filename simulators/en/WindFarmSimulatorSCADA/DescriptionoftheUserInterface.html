Description of the Eolico Park Simulator ›› Simulator components ››

The main synoptic of this simulator has the following parts:

•Theconnectivity of the wind turbines with the Substation with indication of the state of each wind turbine and its current power generated.(Top).

•The simulator of theweather station. (Located on the bottom left).

•The simulator of theProduction demand. (Located at the bottom right).

•The simulator of the display interfacestate and control of wind turbines. (In the central part).

•Indication oftotal power being produced in the park.

•The interface forstate change and efficiency of wind turbines.

•Access to3D display.

•The monitoring of the operation of theautomatic control algorithmsstart / stop of wind turbines.
