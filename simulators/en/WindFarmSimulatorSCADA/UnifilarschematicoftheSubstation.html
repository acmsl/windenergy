LaElectrical substationincludes the transformer that increases the output voltage of the aerogenerators, usually in Low voltage, 690 V, although they also exist with output in Media voltage (between 3KV and 5KV) to the level of the evacuation line, which is usually in High voltage (> 110 KV), although there may be cases of connection in Media voltage (> 30KV).

Generally, depending on the extension of the Park, the output of each wind turbine has a lift transformer (typically from 690 V to 15KV or 20 KV), in order to reduce the loss in the connection cables with the Substation transformer.

In the Substation are thecutting devices(sectionators andSwitches) which allow the transformer to be connected to the evacuation line, and'cells'of operation, which in turn contains the cutting devices that allow to connect and disconnect the cables that connect with the turbine transformers: this functionality is required to be able to perform works that require the deactivation of some of these cables.

Adicionalmente, y para cumplir con las normativas de operación, en la Subestación existen mecanismos de control estandard, que monitorizan: a) la frecuencia de la red; b) que la tensión esté dentro de determinados limites, c) las temperaturas de determinados elementos, etc.

The following figure shows the "Unifilar diagram"corresponding to the substation of the wind farm of example. The concept of" unifilar "corresponds to a simplified representation in which each line in the drawing corresponds to the three (only the three phases), or four (three phases and the neutral) electrical threads of a three-phase line.


At the top the connection with the (aerial in this case) connection with the Electric Network is shown: in this case at 220 KV.

The connections with the wind turbines are concentrated in three lines C-1, C2, and C3. The connection of the wind turbines is made in "daisy chain": from one to the next next. Each of the wind turbines has one of these cables, and internally have devices for their connection / disconnection.

These lines can be in Media voltage (as in this case) with tension between 15KV and 30KV, although there are also wind parks where wind turbines are connected to Low voltage (690V), which is usually the output of the electric generator. The connection in Media Tension empowers the installation (transformers in each aerogenerator to raise from 690V to Media Tension), but reduces the transmission losses.

Between the high voltage output and the connections with the wind turbines are:

•El Sectionator"89 L." This device has two fixed positions: (a) one in which it connects the lower circuit to the Electric Network, and (b) one in which this circuit is set to Earth. Position (b) is used to performmaintenance operationsSubstation. This device cannot be operated(i.e., you cannot change your position from (a) to (b) or vice versa)if current exists or may exist through it. I mean, you can only change your position if the "52 L" switch is open.

•El Switch"52 L" is the main cutting and connection element of the Substation.This device can change status in any situation: that is, it can be opened with the connected Electric Network (closed "89 L" sectionator) and the connected wind turbines (C-1, C-2 and C-3 closed) and in production. It can also be opened in the same circumstances.
