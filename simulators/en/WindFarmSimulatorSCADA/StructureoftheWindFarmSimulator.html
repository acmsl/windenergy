Description of the Eolico Park Simulator

The ACM SL "Eolico Park Simulator," corresponding to LdPv1r12's "WFSSCADAr12.conf" configuration, has been designed to create avirtual spacewhere the basic functions existing in a real wind farm can be exercised, and the consequences of these actions can be observed.

The basic ideas for this simulator are:

•Show thereal-time operation of a wind farm wind turbine set.

•Show thefunctions of the SCADA system of a wind farm:

oAir turbines command.

oCollection of production and state dataof the wind turbines.

oElcommunication system with wind turbines.

oLa interface with the unit of measurement of weather conditions.

oLa visualization of the evolution of different variables in the aerogenerators, environmental and demand.

oSomeexamples of typical control algorithmsused for management.

oLa user interface, through which the operator can exercise these functionalities.

•Functions for thechange in the operating status of wind turbines.

•Functionality foremulate demand.

•3D visualizationfrom the wind farm.

•Meteorological Tower Simulation

The model of wind farm chosen for simulation is composed of 29 aerogenerators of the type of variable pitch, with an input control in aerodynamic losses (Active Stall), similar to that used as a reference in the "Two-Speed Aerogenerator Simulator".

The wind speed in each of them is simulated by a distribution similar to the influence of a real distribution in a territory, from the speed and direction specified in the Meteorological Tower simulator.

The operating status of the wind turbines has been reduced to three: "In Production", "Stop", "Not available"in order to facilitate the general understanding of operations.
