Production Control

Operations in a wind farm ››

As described inIntegration of a wind farm into the Electric NetworkandThe Control Room - SCADA, the basic functionality of the Park is to deliver at all times the required power, which comes to it as a slogan, which depends basically on the forecast of the wind and atmospheric conditions. And this must be done in compliance with the needs imposed by the maintenance of the aerogenerators and other equipment of the park in its optimal condition.

The park operator must ensure that this condition is met. For this purpose it has control of the start-up or end-up of each of the wind turbines.

A graphic representation such as the one shown in the following figure is available in the control room of a wind farm:

Legend: 

•(m / s) wind speed in meters per second.

•Wind Forecast(m / s): evolution of wind speed forecast.

•Means(m / s): average wind speed that has existed.

•External(MW): value of the power delivery to be generated in megawatts.

•PowertOutput(MW): real value of the power produced in megawatts.

The above figure shows the graph with the evolution of the medium wind and the power generated to date 15: 57: 00, together with the wind forecast and the expected generation of date 16: 02: 00.

The power control generated at each moment is done from the SCADA with a procedure that depends on the type of wind turbines in the Park: if they are squirrel cage generators directly connected to the network, the only method of power control is to stop or start wind turbines. This is the case with the above figure. As a result, the average power generated follows with considerable resolve the evolution of the slogan, but with differences. These differences must be within the tolerance of the slogan. If the generators allow for an active power delivery, such as the rotor-controlled aerogenerators, or the double-powered, this power control has much better resolution (it follows the delivery more faithfully).

The start or stop of the aerogenerators, and / or the change of the slogans to each aerogenerator (as the case may be) can be done in "manual mode" or by means of an "automated control mechanism" (seeAutomatic management ) 

In manual mode, the user can "stop" and "start" each aerogenerator, from the SCADA, using a similar functionality as described in "Possible action". 

The criteria for automatic management take into account considerations relating to the ageing of wind turbines, state of the shovels, multipliers, generator, planned maintenance, etc. In general, the operator also has easy-to-use procedures to avoid the application of these criteria to certain wind turbines in certain situations: this functionality is shown in the "Use" column inView of the SCADA application.
