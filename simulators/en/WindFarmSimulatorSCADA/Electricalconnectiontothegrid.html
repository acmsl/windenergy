The power connection of the generator from the aerogenerator to the Network is usually carried out by means of an elevator that passes from the 690V triphasic to the Media voltage (15 KV to 30 KV), although there are generators operating in the Media voltage (5KV). This transformer can be located inside the Tower (case of Spain), or outside, very close to the Tower. In the most powerful wind turbines, the transformer is located in the Gondola. The output of this transformer is connected to the nearest power cable, which connects it to the Substation, to theCommon Connection Point (PCC).

Internally, the connection of power cables varies from one aerogenerator to another. The following figure shows the main components for an aerogenerator with a squirrel cage generator with two possible types of connection (equivalent to having two independent generators).

These elements are inside a cabinet that meets various safety standards (type IP56). Its main components are:

There are often several groups, grouped by panels, according to the arrangement where the other power elements are placed (mainly the Tyrants and their Bypass contactors).

•TheTyrantsand theirBypass contactors: these are the physical components of theSoftstarterused for the operation of connecting the generator to the Network.

•TheCurrent measurement transformers(one per phase).
