1.Review the concepts of the possible states of an aerogenerator (SeeState management of an aerogenerator.)

2.Select an aerogenerator in the SCADA view that is in "Production"or"Stop".

3.Use the "Put 'Out of Service'" button to move your status to "Not available".

4.The aerogenerator shall be maintained as "the selected one," so that the button shall be added to the label "Put 'Active'." By pressing this button now the wind turbine will move to "Stop. "If you want it to go to production you should click on the SCADA section, in your column"Start/Stop".
