La 3D viewis controlled from the tagged checkbox "3D view of the Park".

Make sure you are selected and see how the corresponding synoptic appears (described in3D view ).

As long as it is visible, this synoptic will present a focused view of the aerogenerator that is selected in the sectionSCADA.

In the "Weather station"modify the wind direction by turning the circular button with a red mark. Note that the selected wind generator changes the direction, adapting to the new wind direction.

The response time for wind direction changes in a real wind turbine is quite slow, of the order of 0.5 degrees / second. That is, it takes a full turn of 720 seconds (= 360 / 0.5), that is, 12 minutes. In the simulator, an immediate response has been chosen to avoid these long waiting times.
