Within the Tower is the electric cabinet through which the connection to the Network is made, by means of cables that connect the generator to a transformer. This transformer may be located in the Gondola, or at the base of the Tower, or may be in a nearby outer shed.

In addition, the aerogenerator has a control unit, known asController, in charge of the operation of the same. The Controller may be made up of industrial computers, orPLCs (Programmable Logic Controllers) or combinations thereof. The Controller receives signals from the sensors and commands the different elements to achieve the required functionality at all times. Given the complexity of the functions to be performed, their programming is often carried out using the concept of"State machines. "In general there are two: one that is responsible for the proper operations (cutin, cutout, Gondola orientation, adaptation of the pitch as a function of the wind and the desired power, connection and disconnection of the generator, etc.), and one that monitors the general safety of operation (overvoltage or power, excessive speeds of the Power Train or the electric generator, alarms, excessive temperatures, etc.). Both work in coordination.
