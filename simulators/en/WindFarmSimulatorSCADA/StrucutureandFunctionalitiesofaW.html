Structure and Functions of a wind farm

Introduction ›› What is a wind farm? ››

The elements of a wind farm are:

•TheAerogenerators.

•LaElectrical substationwith which it is connected to the Electric Network.

•El power cable setwhich connect each of the aerogenerators to the Substation.

•El electrical signal measurement system, located in the Substation.

•Themeasuring instruments for weather conditions: wind speed and direction, room temperature, humidity and atmospheric pressure.

•The Park Monitoring and Control System (SCADAEnglishSupervisoryControlAnd DataA).

•Reactive Power Control and Management Elements(condenser banks, STATCOMs, etc.)

•Thecommunication systems between SCADA and aerogenerators.

•ThePark communications systems with other Monitoring and Control centres outside the Park.

The features available within a wind farm have to do with the following topics:

•Operational control of aerogenerators 

•Management of maintenance operations:planned, preventive, corrective.

•Registration of all operations within the Park.

•All management operations required to comply with theelectricity supply contractsthey have agreed with the customers.

Most of these functionalities are usually performed through SCADA.
