Connections of wind turbines to the PCC

Description of the Eolico Park Simulator ›› Simulator components ›› Main synoptic ››

The top of the main synoptic presents a graph with the shape of the connections of the wind turbines to the PCC (Electrical Substation).

The state of the wind turbine is also indicated:

El greenthe aerogenerator indicates that it is in production.

The height of the green column on its left indicates the current production, compared to the nominal.

El yellowindicates that it is not in production, although available, without problems.

El redindicates that the aerogenerator is not available: that is, it presents some problem that prevents it from being put into production, or that it advises not to be put into production.
