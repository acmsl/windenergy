Remote control

Description of the Simulator ›› Emulated Wind Turbine ›› Controller ›› Control modes ››

There are a number of commands that the Wind Turbine responds to, via the connection line that typically is connected to the Wind Farm's SCADA system. These commands depends on the type of SCADA and also on the capabilities of the software in the Wind Turbines Controller.


The wind turbine controller serves commands received from the Control Centre Wind Park. They are generally of two types:

•DataQuery: The SCADA asks the Wind Turbine to send a telegram with the operational data.

•StartStop: The SCADA gives permission to the Wind Turbine to start or order it to have a soft STOP.
