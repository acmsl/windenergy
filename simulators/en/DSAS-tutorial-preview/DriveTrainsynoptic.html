Drive Train synoptic

Description of the Simulator ›› Synoptics ››

This SynopticDriveTrain synoptic has been designed to present  the most important signals that arrive to the Nacelle emulator as well as signals out from the Nacelle emulator.


The listing of these signals is the following:


•The Rotor speed (rpms), from the Nacelle emulator.

•The Generator speed (rpms), from the Nacelle emulator.

•The Pitch value (º), from the Controller emulator.

•The Hydraulic circuit pressure of the braking system, from Nacelle emulator

•The Hydraulic circuit Pump status, from the Controller emulator.

•The state of the valve in the hydraulic circuit, controlled by the "Soft brake" and "Hard brake" coming from the Controller emulator.

•The status of the pressure switch in the hydraulic circuit.

•The brakes status (released or not) from the Nacelle emulator.

•The state of each switches for the connection of the Generator (Large : 4 poles and Small : 6 poles), from the Controller emulator.

•The state of the overspeed sensors (VCU, HCU)

•The state of the pole's vibrations sensor.

•The temperatures for the ambient (setting), Rotor's bearing, the Gearbox and the Generator from the Nacelle emulator.


It has two main "Views" controlled through the top menu: one showing the internal elements in the Nacelle and another that present a view of the Wind Turbine in the Wind Farm.
