Absolute encoders

What is a Wind Turbine ›› Structure of a Wind Turbine ›› Auxiliary sensors ›› Angular encoders ››

The absolute encoders contain as many slotted circular annulus as required for their resolution.

For example, a absolute encoder that is able to resolve better than 1 degree must have at least 9 slots, as 9 is the first integer such as 29 > 360.

Each slot is has its own set of light emitter and sensor. The status of this sensors contains the absolute angular position: there is no need for an external counter.


There is an special arrangement of the coding of the slots that has the property of changing only one of the channels for a minimal change on the shaft angle: it is named after his inventor, "Grey coding".

The main advantage of this coding of the plate is that it facilitates the error checking of the measurements.
