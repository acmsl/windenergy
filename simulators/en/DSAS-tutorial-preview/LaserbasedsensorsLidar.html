Ultrasonic sensors

What is a Wind Turbine ›› Structure of a Wind Turbine ›› Meteorological sensors ›› Combined sensors ››

There is a supplier of a device that is placed and the spinner (nose of the hub), that uses ultrasounds to measure the speed and turbulence of the wind flow, in the front of the rotor. It also measures flow inclination, air pressure and the ambient temperature. Therefore it is easy to compute the air density, a factor that influence the power vs wind speed curve of the Wind Tubines.

Using software processing it can provide also Nacelle orientation and Rotor position.

According to the manufacturers, the use of this type of sensor greatly increments the accuracy of the wind measurements, as is depicted in the following pictures.
