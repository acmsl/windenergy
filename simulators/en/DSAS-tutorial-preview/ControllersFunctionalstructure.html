Controller's Functional structure

Description of the Simulator ›› Emulated Wind Turbine ›› Controller ››

The Controller in an actual Wind Turbine is the set of electronic and electrical components that controls the behavior of the different subsystem in the Wind Turbine, including mainly the Nacelle subsystems as well as the electrical panel elements, so that the overall behavior of the Wind Turbine meets the planned behavior of producing the maximum energy out of the wind during most part of the time.

For that the Controller (some times implemented on a PLC: programmable logic controller, or using an Industrial grade PC) has embedded algorithms to control each subsystem (hydraulic, mechanic, electrical, security) and to orchestrate them in order to achieve its target.
