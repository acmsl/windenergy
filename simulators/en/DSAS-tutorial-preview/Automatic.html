Automatic

Description of the Simulator ›› Emulated Wind Turbine ›› Controller ›› Control modes ››

The Automatic mode is the normal mode of operation of the Wind Turbine. The principle is that the Controller is in charge of monitoring all the Nacelle and Grid variables as well as the wind, and, if everything is Ok, keep waiting for two commands: "START" or "STOP". These commands can be issued by the service personnel through the Control Terminal, or can be issued remotely from the SCADA of the Wind Farm.


Upon receipt of the START command, check the value and direction of the wind. If the wind speed is sufficient to cutin, and is less than the maximum wind speed for cutout, then try to orient the nacelle into the wind, and begin the process of cutin, so that in the end, the turbine shall be connected to the network and producing.
