Requirements imposed by the Electric Generator

The Dual Speed Active Stall Wind Turbine uses an squirrel cage electric generator directly connected to the Grid. The connection is done using an Soft-starter circuit that is Bypassed in production. The concept of "Dual Speed" is achieved using two combinations of the stator windings, such as with one of them the generator has 4 magnetic poles, and with the other 6 magnetic poles.
