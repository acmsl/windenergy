Bimetal sensors

What is a Wind Turbine ›› Structure of a Wind Turbine ›› Auxiliary sensors ›› Temperature sensors ››

These sensors use a bimetallic strip to convert a temperature change into a displacement.

They are very rugged and normally are used for sensing that the temperature of some component reaches a fixed value: when it gets above, the circuit change its state (opening normally).
