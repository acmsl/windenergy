Connection with the Control Center SCADA

What is a Wind Turbine ››

From the point of view of operation, each Wind Turbine is a power generation plant on its own, and as such, works independently of the Wind Farm SCADA.

But, It  is responsive to inquiries from the SCADA (in the Control Room of the Wind Farm). The SCADA uses specific protocols to request information from the Wind Turbine: this information is related to the operation of the Wind Turbine, such as total generated energy, evolution of active and reactive power, evolution of wind speed, temperatures, etc), and about the general situation of the Wind Turbine: alarms, incidences, etc. Also, the Wind Turbine Controller can accept and execute "commands" issued from the SCADA, such as "START" and "STOP", or setting of the active or reactive power to generate. 

An important aspect of the Wind Turbine Controller is the generation of alarms and error messages that may occur during the operation of the Wind Turbine: collectively they are named "incidences" in the Simulator.
