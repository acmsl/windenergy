What can you learn with this Simulator?

Introduction ››

Using this simulator you can:

•Learn about the fundamentals and basic structure of a Wind Turbine.

•Learn about the description of its components, their  physical principal, and their behaviors.

•Observe the evolution of its internal variables when it enters production and leaves it.

•Observe the reaction of its internal variables to changes in its operating conditions, both normal, exceptional or with faults.

This simulator is based on a type of wind turbine that was widespread until the early 2000s, which uses a squirrel-cage electric generator, and can change the angle of attack of the blades (pitch).

This tutorial is an integral part of the simulator and is organized into the following sections:

•What is a Wind Turbine?

In this section you will find an introduction to the components of a typical wind turbine, along with the specifics of the family called "Active Stall".

•Physics of a Wind Turbine

Here you will find a description of the fundamentals of the subsystems in a generic wind turbine, and specific data related to industrial wind turbines.

•Description of the Simulator

This section describes the structure and components of this simulator. As it runs on the LdP platform, it can also be used as a Test Bench, where the parameters of the different subsystems can be changed to observe various behaviors. You will need to know the concepts about the LdP platform, that you can find in the "Introduction to LdP" tutorial.
