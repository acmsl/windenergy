The simplified general unifilar schematic of the electrical wiring of the Wind Turbine is sketched in the next figure.



The Generator and its "Large" and "Small" connection switches are placed at the Nacelle.

The remaining parts are located in the Electrical Panel typically in the base of the tower. 

In the type of Wind Turbine we have chosen for this Simulator the actual connection of the Grid to the Generator is done firstly using controlling the conduction angle of the blocks of  thyristors (or IGBTs) for the Cutin operations, later "bypassed", using normal high current switches in order to reduce the losses in the thyristors (and hence their working life).

In the Electrical Panel you can find also the current measurement transformers connected to the Wind Turbines Controller for power and energy measurements and also for security and protection algorithms.
