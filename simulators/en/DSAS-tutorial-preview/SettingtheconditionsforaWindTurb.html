Setting the conditions for a Wind Turbine to start operation with low wind speed

Teaching sessions ››

1.Start the simulator: "Executing"-->"Run".

2.Observe that the Pitch is at -90º a little after receiving the command from Controller.


3.Go to the "Control Terminal" and check in the middle tab ("Opened incidences") that there is no one pending. If you get a "Manual STOP", just acknowledge it (double click in the "Ack" column) and then press the "START" button). If it is the first time, the configuration does not set automatically the "Main Contactor" to On ("Electrical Panel"-->"Electrical Panel"-->. Click on the handle to set it "On". A voltage loss is an incidence that must be recognized by the Operator. So you must go to the Wind Turbine, plug your Control Terminal, look for this problem and "acknowledge" each one of these incidences. You will see that they dissapears from "Opened incidences" and appears in "Historic incidencies", as shown in next figure.

 

You should have the following listing:


Check the "Ack" check-box and press "START"

4.Go to the SynopticWindNacelle synoptic  and set a wind speed to any value below 6.2 m/s. We want the Wind Turbine to do a cutin with an small wind.


5.Play with the wind direction (note: do not try to change it very rapidly the direction of the wind, do it with the Arrow Keys "Up" and "Down"). Check that, after some time, the Controller issues the appropriate commands so the Nacelle is oriented in the same direction as the wind. You will see "Yaw CCW" and "Yaw CW" highlighted while the corresponding motion is active.

6.Check the "Effective wind" in the "Control Terminal " synoptic. This is due to misalignment between the wind direction and the Nacelle orientation. The "Effective wind" value will approach the "wind speed" you set up in SynopticWindNacelle synoptic as the angular difference diminishes.

7.Check that the orientation of the Nacelle does not reach the exact value of the wind direction.That's is on purpose. See explanation in Yawing.
Check that the "Windvane" widget presents a signal that changes from a continuous value (staying high or low) to an oscillating signal. There is some randomness in duty cycle of that signal, mimicking the real wind vane signal.


8.If a "StripRecorder" with Panel definition "Cutin+Cutout+Production" is not present, go to the "Executing .." synoptic -->"Synoptics"-->"StripRecorder"; then, when the StripRecorder appears press "Predefined panels" and choose "Cutin+Cutout+Production". This panel presents 6 recorders, with the following variables on them:

1.Controller.Control.PitchOut and Nacelle.DriveTrain.PitchNacelleOut. With this recorder you can appreciate the difference between the value of the Pitch Set Point sent by the Controller and the real Pitch in the Nacelle.

2.Nacelle.DriveTrain.GeneratedPowerOut. The power delivered to the Grid.

3.Nacelle.DriveTrain.ThyristorsIn , Nacelle.Braking.BrakeReleasedOut and Nacelle.DriveTrain.ByPassIn : these are digital (boolean) signals. In this recorder you may see the relationship between these key signals, very important in the cutin process. They are a little bit displaced, so that when more than one signal have the same value they still can be seen.

4.Nacelle.DriveTrain.GeneratorRPMsOut : the speed of the Generator shaft.

5.SynopticWindNacelle.WindDirectionOut , SynopticWindNacelle.NacelleOrientationOut : this recorder visualize the evolution of these signal related to the yawing algorithm.

6.SynopticWindNacelle.WindSpeedOut : this recorder visualize the evolution you impressed in the wind speed.


You can change the "sampling rate". Any value between "0,2s" (five samples per second)  to "1s" (one sample per second) is ok for this case.
At any moment you can press the "Hardcopy" menu option that will bring an independent form that contains a "frozen" view of the recorders at the time of pressing it. It that form you get a time cursor that gives you the instants when events happened, and also you can measure time distances using the pop-up menu that is shown pressing the right button on the mouse.

9.Go to the "Control Terminal" and press the green "START" button. You can observe how is the evolution of the different signals in several synoptics:

1.Blades rpms in the SynopticDriveTrain, the Control Terminal and the StripRecorder

2.The Generator's rpms in the Control Terminal and StripRecorder

3.Nacelle orientation in the SynopticWindNacelle and Control Terminal

4.Pressure in the hydraulic brake circuit in the SynopticDriveTrain

5.Blades pitch in SynopticDriveTrain (both "Internal View" and "Farm View"), in the Control Terminal and in the SynopticWindNacelle.

6.Actual wind of the Blades in the SynopticWindNacelle

7.Status of the Bypass in the "Electrical Panel", both in the "external" and "internal" views.

10.You can observe that the sequence is:

1.Setting the Generator to "Small Connection" (6 poles).

2.Setting the brake (for checking its operational state)

3.Brake release.

4.Ramp up of the rpms.

5.Controlled ramp up in the RPMs to keep acceleration within limits.

6.Approach to synchronous speed (should be 1000 rpms), connection of the Thyristors, some reactive consumption.

7.Connection of the Bypass , removal of Thyristors, enter production stage.
Meanwhile the pressure of the brake hydraulic circuit will move according to some preset pressure losses, and the Nacelle following any changes in the wind direction.
