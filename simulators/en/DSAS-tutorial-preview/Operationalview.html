What is a Wind Turbine ››

The Controller of the Wind Turbine is programmed to maximize the production out the existing wind speed, and for that, it has all the measuring sensors needed to evaluate the wind availability.

From the point of view of operation, we can think of a Wind Turbine as a set of the following subsystems:

•The mechanical, electrical, hydraulic subsystems, typically located in the Nacelle and along the tower, as mentioned above.

•A Controller unit, a electronic subsystem that takes care of implementing all the algorithms needed for the adequate functioning of the Wind Turbine as a whole, receiving inputs from the subsystems in the Nacelle, providing control signals for them and receiving commands from the Operator through a computer terminal, either local or remote (SCADAs), and providing information about the operational and exploitation details of the Wind Turbine.

•A Command terminal, through which an Operator can govern the Wind Turbine.
