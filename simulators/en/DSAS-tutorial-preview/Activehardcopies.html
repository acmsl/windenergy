Active hardcopies


These are independent synoptics that presents the snapshot of the strip-recorders at the moment of hardcopy's generation. See next figure:


They present the following functionalities:

•A Time Cursor  (light blue vertical line): moving the cursor the corresponding instant time is shown in the "Actual" widget in the top left corner.

•A pop-up menu available through the right mouse button, allow the setting of a "Reference Time" (yellow line in the figure). Once the Reference time set, the movement of the Time Cursor updates also the time distance between the current Time Cursor position and the Reference Time.
