Ultrasonic sensors

What is a Wind Turbine ›› Structure of a Wind Turbine ›› Meteorological sensors ›› Wind speed: Anemometers ››

The ultrasonic anemometer is based on the concept that wind speed modifies the sound speed transmission. They use two orthogonal sound emitter and receiver pairs, as sensors to compute the wind speed, using an algorithm that takes into account that these two traversing times depend on the sound velocity on the air, that depends on the wind speed.
