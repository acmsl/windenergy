Yaw system

What is a Wind Turbine ›› Structure of a Wind Turbine ››

The Yaw system implements the following functionalities:

•Orienting the Nacelle to the wind direction. These is achieved with a number of motors (typically two sets of motor rotating the Nacelle in each direction, clockwise (CW) and counterclockwise (CCW).

•Keeping the Nacelle firmly attached to the Tower when its orientation is ok for the existing wind direction. This is achieved with some pads that increments the friction with the Tower.

•Detecting and controlling the actual number of turns of the power cables. These cables fall from the Nacelle freely, so they solve the problem of connecting the Grid (at the bottom of the Tower) to a eventually turning provider: the Generator that may change position due to Nacelle orientation. 

As the cables may twist along its longitudinal axis, they solve that problem. But there is a limit to that twist: this limit is detected typically with a limit switch fixed to the Tower, and with its actuator tip attached to the power cables envelop.
