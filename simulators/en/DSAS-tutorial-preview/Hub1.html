The Hub is the element of the Wind Turbine where the Blades are attached and that contains the mechanism governing the pitch at each blade.

In a real Wind Turbine, the Pitch mechanisms may be only one, common to all  the Blades, or may have one for each of them. See Pitch mechanism.

The rate of change of the Pitch of these mechanisms is the key characteristic to be taken into account for the modeling of the dynamics of the DriveTrain. Therefore, the NacelleEmulator emulates this mechanism assuming:

a) they comply with the requirements for the range of angles they must span (otherwise they will not be part of the Wind Turbine), and 

b)they may present problems in production that can be modeled with a low performance in the rate of change of the angle. Thus the important parameter for the Pitch mechanism is its maximum slew-rate as response to a step change in the commanded angle.  See next figure. The black line represents an ideal command changed from the Controller. 


The parameter used for the pitch mechanism (slew rate coefficient) can be modified with the parameter: Changing this parameter it is possible to simulate faults in the pitch mechanism  or in its connection with the Controller. For example, if this parameter value is 0 then the pitch of the Blades will not change when the Controller tries to change it, generating the corresponding incidence.

Setting it to a low value can simulate a problem such excessive friction in the mechanical part of the pitch mechanism.
