Yaw brakes

What is a Wind Turbine ›› Structure of a Wind Turbine ›› Yaw system ››

Yaw brakes are used to maintain a fixed position of the Nacelle when the difference of its orientation and the wind direction is below a certain value. They help on reducing efforts on the Yaw motors pinions, as well as on the bearings and supports of the DriveTrain elements in the Nacelle.
