Brake's disc

Description of the Simulator ›› Emulated Wind Turbine ›› Processes in NacelleEmulator ›› Drive Train ››

The Wind Turbine method for stopping the DriveTrain, in normal operation, is controlling the Pitch. Setting this value far from 0º (like airplane's wings are landing) has the effect that the kinetic energy in the DriveTrain is used to impulse the air at the Blades, slowing their rotational speed. This method has no wear.  Nevertheless, the Wind Turbine includes a mechanical disc brake that actually is used, typically, for two purposes:


•To keep the DriveTrain fixed during maintenance operations.

•To act as a Security stop mechanism, before the other two extreme mechanisms (VCU and HCU) may enter in operation.


The reason for this approach is that it would be very cumbersome install a disc brake that could dissipate (internally to the Nacelle) all the kinetic energy of the DriveTrain, especially on big Wind Turbines (above 1MW). So all the protocols used in the Controller to stop the Blades try to minimize the use of the disc brake.


In the Dual Speed Active Stall Wind Turbine Simulator we use several levels of Stop Sequences to mimic this behavior: levels 1 to 3 use the disc brake.


The disc brake is normally set during  the initial phase of the cutin operation (see or monitor the signal Nacelle.BrakeReleased). This is done as a precautionary step, so that the Controller can sense its operational status,  before letting the Blades start turning.
