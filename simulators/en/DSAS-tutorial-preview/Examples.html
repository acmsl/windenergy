The main reason for having independent Pitch angle is to compensate for the passing of the Blades in front of the Tower, and to equalize the Torque contribution as they rotate counteracting wind shear. The benefits are lower stress in the Blades and better Power Quality: without the compensation for Pitch as the Blade crosses in front of the Tower contribute largely to the "Flicker" of the delivered energy.
