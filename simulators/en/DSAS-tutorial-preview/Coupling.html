Coupling

Description of the Simulator ›› Synoptics ›› Drive Train synoptic ›› Components descriptions ››

The coupling element is shown in a similar way as the disc brake, mimicking a real element.

It turns at the same speed of the generator and the upper shaft of the gearbox.

Its  purpose is to compensate the different alignment of the Generator and Gearbox shafts.
