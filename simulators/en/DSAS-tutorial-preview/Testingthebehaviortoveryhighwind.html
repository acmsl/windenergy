Testing the behavior to very high winds

Teaching sessions ›› Behavior facing very high wind speeds ››

1.Start the application.

2.Go the the "WTElectricalPanel" and set the "Main Switch" to the On position. 

3.Go to the "Executing" form and press "Run". In "Synoptic control"  un-check the "WTElectricalPanel", is not necessary in this lesson. Rearrange the synoptics in the screen.

4.Go to the "Control Terminal" recognize any pending incidences and press the "START" button.

5.In the "SynopticWindNacelle", using PgUp set a wind speed of 15 m/s, in order to start de operation in Large.
NB. As in other sessions we will use specific values of wind, you should guide the wind direction with the Nacelle to get a perfect matching between the effective wind and the value introduced in the wind widget.

6.Connected in large with a wind of 15 m/s with the default parameters the "RPMs Gen" should be around  1509,5 rpms and producing near the nominal power of 1300kW. 

7.Using the PgUp key set the wind at 26 m/s, that is above WindMaxForOperation [25m/s] but not over  WindMaxForTemporalOperation [28m/s].

8.Wait 20 seconds and the incidence "Wind > WindMaxForOperation for more than 100 s" will appear in the "Control Terminal" "Open Incidences" tab.


Note: remember that some time delays have been scaled to make the simulator more usable.

9.Set the wind at 24 m/s, and observe that the incidence passes to the historic record without Operator  acknowledgment. This kind of wind incidences doesn't need acknowledgment by the user: they disappear as soon as the cause disappear. 

10.Set the wind at 20 m/s, below MaxWindForCutin [21 m/s], and the Wind Turbine will start a cut-in sequence.

11.Repeat the test with winds above  WindMaxForTemporalOperation [28m/s] and WindAbsolutMax [30m/s]. You will get the incidences: 
"Wind > WindMaxForTemporalOperation for more than 5 s" 
"Wind > WindAbsolutMax for more that 1 s"
respectively. The last one is practically instantaneous (1 s.)  
 

12.Finally, set the wind down to 15 m / s and verify that all issues go to the historical record, and the turbine starts up again at its rated power.
