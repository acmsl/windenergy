While in production, change from high to low wind speed

Teaching sessions ››

Repeat experiment While in production, change from low wind speed to high wind speed. and decrease the wind slowly from high wind (17 m/s) down to 6 m/s. You should be able to get an StripRecorder Hardcopy as shown in next figure.

1.You can now do the contrary operation: being in "Large Connection" pass to "Small Connection". To achieve that just reduce the the wind speed below 6.2 m/s. This is the sequence of what will happen:

1.The Bypass will be set to Off, consequently Production will drop to 0.

2.The Pitch will pass to -15º.

3.Generator rpms will drop till reaching a value below synchronous speed at 6 poles (1.000 rpms): around 950 rms.

4.At that moment Pitch will grow again (depend on the wind speed).

5.Approaching 1000 rpms the Thyristors will start conducting again.

6.After 3 seconds (approx.) the Bypass will be set On again, so the Wind Turbine will be again in production.

2.STOPing the machine (cutout). This is done with the red "STOP" button in the "Control Terminal". It is operative will the Wind Turbine is in production.

3.Provoke an electrical fault. Go to the "WTElectrical Panel" and uncheck any of the checkboxes associated with the Grid phases, or set the Main Switch to Off. The Controller will initiate a fast should down, bringing the rpms to 0 in short time and letting the State Machine in the "SFF" state.


Again, a very short time since the Controller decides to go to Small Connection and the Wind Turbine is producing again.
