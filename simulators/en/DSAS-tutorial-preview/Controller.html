Controller

Description of the Simulator ›› Emulated Wind Turbine ››

The Controller part of the DSAS Simulator includes all the controlling algorithms used to:

•monitor all the signals coming from the simulated mechanical, electrical, thermal, etc. processes in the Nacelle, 

•control the elements on that systems in order to implement the overall functionalities, i.e., produce electric energy, keeping the working conditions under all the safety requirements.
