🎯 Cable unwinding

Objective: To understand why it is necessary to control cable twisting in orientation (yaw).

What to observe: Accumulated yaw position and event that triggers unwinding.

What to note: Condition that triggers correction and system behavior during the process.

💡 Relate repeated orientation changes to accumulated twisting in the wiring.

Power cable unwinding

Practical classes ›› Effects of wind direction change ››

The objective of this practical is to check the handling of power cable unwinding. Read about the basis for expected behavior in Algorithms for controlling the orientation and unwinding of power cables.  

The concepts to be verified are as follows:

1. If the wind direction changes while the generator is connected to the grid (in production), the controller monitors the number of turns of the power cables: if it reaches the value indicated by the parameter "ControllerYawing.MaxCableTwist" (default, 2.5), the Controller initiates an unwinding process, which involves first disconnecting the generator from the grid (cutout), and finally reconnecting it (cutin).

2. If the generator is disconnected from the grid and a condition arises for reconnection to the grid (cutin), either because the wind has picked up to a sufficient level or because the operator has given the "START" command, and the number of turns of the power cables is greater than the value of the parameter "ControllerYawing.MaxCableTwistBeforeCutin" (default, 1.5), then the Controller initiates the unwinding process and then continues with the connection process (cutin).

For 1):

•Ensure that the generator is connected to the grid (in production).

•Change the wind direction in the same direction until the limit of 2.5 turns of the power cables is reached. To change the wind direction, in the "SynopticWindNacelle" synoptic, "Wind direction" indicator, click with the mouse on the value (white background), and use the "Page Forward," "Page Back," "Up Arrow," and "Down Arrow" keys gently (one press per second, for example) and observe how the nacelle orientation "follows" the new wind direction value.

•Note that when the new orientation causes the number of turns of the power cables to exceed 2.4, the controller will initiate the process of disconnecting the generator from the grid (cutout) and will proceed to rotate the nacelle in the direction that decreases the turns, until this value is within 0.5 turns.

For 2):

•Ensure that the generator is disconnected from the grid (stopped). Press "STOP" if necessary and wait.

•Using one of the "Manual rotation" buttons on the "Control Terminal" screen, force the rotation, always in the same direction, until the number of revolutions of the power cables is above 1.5.

•Ensure that there are no open incidents, apart from "Manual STOP." Click on the "Ok" column for this incident and press "START."

•Note that the nacelle does not seek the wind direction, but begins to unwind the cables. When the turns are in the range of 0.5 (in absolute value), the operation of orienting and connecting the generator to the grid begins.


In this practice, you can use two recorders, one with the "Yawing" panel and the other with the "Cutin + Cutout + Production" panel to observe the evolution of the relevant signals.
