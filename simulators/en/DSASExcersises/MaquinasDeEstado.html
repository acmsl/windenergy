🎯 State Machines

Objective: To understand how a state machine works and how it describes the operational behavior of the wind turbine.

What to observe: Main states, transitions, input/output conditions, and events (alarms, permissions, setpoints).

What to note: For each transition: source state, destination state, and condition that triggers it.

💡 Draw a mini-diagram with 4–6 key states and their transition arrows.
