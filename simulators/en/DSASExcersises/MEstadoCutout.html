🎯 State machine — Cutout

Objective: To understand the protection sequence that leads to shutdown when the wind exceeds a limit (cutout).

What to observe: States prior to shutdown, triggering event, protection actions, and final safe state.

What to note: Approximate threshold, final state, and whether there is a reset or hysteresis condition.

💡 Distinguish between power limitation and safety shutdown.

❓ Guiding questions

  1. What states does the state machine go through from production to complete shutdown?
  2. In what state does power export to the grid stop?
