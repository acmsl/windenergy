Observation of the effect of a malfunctioning pump

Practical classes ›› Monitoring the hydraulic brake circuit ››

This practical session aims to show how the protocol implemented by the controller detects that the pump has a problem. The pump may have several problems:

a) It may have 'pressure losses' when it is not activated. This case is presented in the class Observation of excessive pressure loss in the circuit.

b) It may have lost thrust capacity, meaning that the flow rate is not as it should be when activated.

c) There may also be electrical problems, such as a blown fuse.

d) etc.

In the wind turbine on which this simulator is based, the mechanism for detecting normal behavior of the pump and its accessories is detected by the time it takes to raise the pressure in the hydraulic circuit above the green zone corresponding to the pressure switch. This method does not distinguish between all possible causes; however, it is a very reliable procedure and does not use sensors that need to be calibrated.

In this simulator, all these problems are emulated using the "NacelleBrake.PumpCaudal" parameter. 

 

Fig. 1.

You must ensure that the wind turbine is in production (connected to the grid) and observe the "Brake circuit pressure" indicator in the "SynopticDriveTrain" synoptic. Also, make sure you have a recorder with the "Hydraulic brake circuit" panel.

To create the fault situation, we can decrease the default value (5 out of a maximum of 100) to a value 50 times lower (0.1). To change the value, follow the instructions described in Changing the value of a parameter.

You will see that the Controller issues an incident with the description "Pumping time too long" and disconnects the generator from the grid. This occurs after a considerable amount of time, as the pressure in the hydraulic circuit must fall below the lower value of the green zone for the Controller to activate the pump.
