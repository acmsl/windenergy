🎯 Why the blades rotate

Objective: To relate wind energy to the generation of aerodynamic torque and the start of rotor rotation.

What to observe: Wind, rotor speed, torque (if available), and turbine status during the start of movement.

What to note: Wind conditions at which the rotor begins to turn and how the speed changes as the wind increases.

💡 Compare behavior in steady wind vs. slow variations to see the rotor's response.

Explanation of the conditions that cause the blades to rotate

Practical classes ››

In this practical, the idea is to observe the concept of "total wind" incident on the edge of the blade, both in intensity and in direction relative to the pitch angle of the blades.

The aim is to observe how the power obtained from the wind is controlled, as explained in Why do the blades rotate? , and to see how small variations in pitch cause the blade to stall, resulting in:

•If in production: the variation in power obtained from the wind 

•If it is in a braking operation, as this power is delivered to the wind.
