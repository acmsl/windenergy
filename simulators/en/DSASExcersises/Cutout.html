🎯 Cut-out (high wind shutdown)

Objective: Identify the safety condition that causes the high wind shutdown and describe the shutdown sequence.

What to observe: Wind speed, alarms/protections, status changes, and control performance.

What to note: Approximate trip threshold, final system status, and conditions for returning to operation.

💡 Check whether the turbine enters power limitation first.

❓ Guiding questions

  1. How long does it take from pressing Stop (grid connection and in production) until the blades stop?
  2. In the same situation, how long does it take for power to stop being exported to the grid?

Observation of the reaction to the "STOP" command

Practical classes ››

This practical class involves observing the evolution of important signals when the generator is disconnected from the grid (cutout). The cutout operation can be triggered manually from the "Control Terminal" using the "STOP" button.

The cutout operation is triggered when the wind turbine is in production (with the generator connected to the grid) and the "STOP" button is pressed on the "Control Terminal" screen, ending with the generator being disconnected from the grid in an orderly manner.
