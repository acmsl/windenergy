🎯 Observation of a fault caused by excessive losses

Objective: To analyze a fault caused by excessive losses in the system.

What to observe: Variables that destabilize first and alarms generated.

What to note: Evidence that allows us to conclude that the origin is excessive loss.

💡 Look for consistency between pressure, flow, and actuator response.

❓ Guiding questions

  1. When a shutdown process is initiated during production, is the hydraulic brake used?
  2. What criteria does the controller use to detect a problem in the hydraulic system and what variables does it monitor?

Observation of normal operation

Practical classes ›› Monitoring the hydraulic brake circuit ››

This practical exercise involves observing the behavior of the brake circuit in a normal situation.

Make sure that the wind turbine is in production (connected to the grid) and observe the "Brake circuit pressure" indicator in the "SynopticDriveTrain" synoptic. Also, make sure you have a recorder with the "Hydraulic brake circuit" panel.

Note that the pressure in the hydraulic circuit remains in the green zone, with the following evolution.

•While the pump is not active, the pressure drops very slowly (corresponding to a circuit with very little pressure loss) until its value falls below the minimum value of the green zone.

•When the value falls below the minimum value of the green zone, the controller starts the pump, which quickly raises the pressure.

•When the pressure exceeds the maximum value of the green zone, the controller stops the pump.
