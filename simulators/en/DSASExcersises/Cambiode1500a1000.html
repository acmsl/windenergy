🎯 Change from 1500 to 1000

Objective: To understand the reverse transition and why the system returns to 1000.

What to observe: Return condition, stability, states, and power/speed evolution.

What to note: Return threshold and time to steady state.

💡 Check whether the return is for safety, efficiency, or speed control.

❓ Guiding questions

  1. How long does it take for the generator/mode to change from high wind (> 7 m/s) to low wind (< 6.2 m/s)?
  2. What variable or condition marks the exact moment of the change?

While in production, wind modification to change the generator connection from large to small

Practical classes ››

This practical class performs an operation similar to that described in During production, modifying the wind to change the connection from Small to Large Generator, but with a wind speed evolution from higher to lower.

Use the "Page forward" key to reduce the wind speed below 6.2 m/s, which corresponds to the "Min for large generator" value (see Functional wind values). For example, leave it at 6 m/s.

On a recorder with the "Cutin + Cutout + Production" panel, you will see the following evolution of the signals.

You can see how the grid is first disconnected (cutout), preceded by a change in the pitch value that reduces the power obtained from the wind. Once the Thyristor Bypass Contactors have been deactivated, the generator connection type changes from 4 poles (large generator) to 6 poles (small generator). The pitch value causes the power train to slow down, which continues until the generator shaft speed "falls" below the synchronous speed (now 1,000 rpm). From that moment on, the controller follows a normal grid connection process (cutin).
