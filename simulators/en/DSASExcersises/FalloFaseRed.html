🎯 Network phase failure

Objective: To understand how the system behaves when a network phase is lost or fails.

What to observe: Electrical alarms, generator/converter status, and transition to safe mode.

What to note: Final status, type of alarm, and conditions necessary for resetting.

💡 Relate the failure to the electrical protection mechanisms.

Observation of the reaction to a failure in one of the network phases

Practical classes ››

In this practical session, the objective is to observe the controller's reaction to a network fault.

View the "Electrical cabinet" diagram.

The three checkboxes under "Thyristor bypass" allow you to "open/close" each of the three phases of the grid.

Set the wind turbine to production (see Setting the conditions for entry into production with low wind).

Change the status of any of these three checkboxes and observe that the Controller initiates, with a delay of a few seconds, a process to disconnect the generator from the network (cutout). 

Incidents related to this protocol will appear in the "Terminal Control" synoptic. See the following figure.

If the phase is restored, the dates in the "Recovered" column will be filled in. These incidents must be "acknowledged" manually by the operator.
