What is a wind turbine? ›› Structure of a horizontal axis wind turbine ›› Orientation mechanism (yaw) ››

The nacelle orientation mechanism incorporates a position locking system designed to prevent the orientation from changing due to:

•The mechanical torque produced by the asymmetry of the wind around the tower.

•The mechanical torque produced by changes in the rotational speed of the blades, through the gyroscopic effect.

The torque created by this gyroscopic effect is perpendicular to the rotation axis of the blades and can be very significant if there is a sudden change in the rotation speed of the blades.
