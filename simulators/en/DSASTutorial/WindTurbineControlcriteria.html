Wind Turbine Design and Control Criteria

Physical Principles ››

This chapter describes the most important concepts that are taken into account in the design of a wind turbine, which begins by considering the characteristics of wind distribution in the area where it will be installed and the choice of the procedure for controlling its stability and safety.

It also provides an introduction to induction electric generators, which are the most commonly used type.
