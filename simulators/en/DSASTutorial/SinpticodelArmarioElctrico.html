Wind Turbine Simulator with Active Stall and Two Speeds ›› Synoptics used in this simulator ›› Electrical Cabinet Synoptic ››

This synoptic shows the exterior view of the electrical cabinet, as shown in the following figure.

It shows:

•The "Main Switch." This graphic indicator is active, so that clicking on it with the left mouse button changes its status (connected/disconnected). The status is indicated in the indicator immediately above.

•The checkbox to switch to the "Internal View."

•The status of the thyristor bypass contactors.

•The indication of the current connection of the generator to the grid. It is indicated as "High speed" and "Low speed," which correspond to the two possible modes: 4-pole and 6-pole, respectively.

•Three checkboxes to control the three phases of the grid, located below the Bypass indicator.
