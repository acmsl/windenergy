Algorithms used

Wind Turbine Simulator with Active Stall and Two Speeds ›› Scope of Simulation ›› Controller ››

The algorithms used correspond to:

•Blade angle control. This affects the functions of adaptation to existing wind conditions, start-up (cutin) and shutdown (cutout) procedures, and safety stop procedures.

•Control of pressure in the hydraulic brake circuit. Affects control of the pump and brake valves.

•Control of the nacelle orientation. Controls the orientation motors.

•Control of power cable unwinding. Controls the orientation motors.

•Monitoring of grid values.

•Monitoring of events that affect the safety of the operation.
