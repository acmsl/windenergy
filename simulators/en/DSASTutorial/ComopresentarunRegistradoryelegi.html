'Cutin + Cutout + Production' Panel

The following figure shows a recorder with the panel called "Cutin + Cutout + Production."


1. Two signals are presented in the first 'channel':

1.Signal generated in the Controller and used as the setpoint value for the blade pitch.

2. Signal from the hub simulator, with the actual blade pitch value.

2. In the second 'channel', a single signal is presented, corresponding to the electrical power generated.

3. Three digital signals are presented in the third 'channel':

1. The thyristor activation signal.

2. The 'brakes released' signal.

3. The signal to activate the thyristor bypass contactor.

4. The fourth 'channel' presents a single signal with the rotational speed of the electric generator shaft (in rpm).

5. The fifth 'channel' presents two signals:

1. Wind direction.

2. The orientation of the nacelle.

6. The sixth 'channel' displays two signals:

1. Wind speed.

2. Effective wind speed.

This panel is very useful for studying the evolution of the wind turbine during grid connection (cut-in), grid disconnection (cut-out), wind direction change, and wind speed change operations.
