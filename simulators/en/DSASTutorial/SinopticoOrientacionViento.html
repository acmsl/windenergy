Synoptic Wind Nacelle Orientation (SynopticWindNacelle)

Wind Turbine Simulator with Active Stall and Two Speeds ›› Synoptics used in this simulator ››

This synoptic allows you to:

•Change the wind speed and direction.

•Display the nacelle orientation.

•Display the number of turns of the power cables.

•Display the wind vane signal. 

•Display the status of the orientation motors. "Left Turn" and "Right Turn."

•Display the existing orientation error (left panel, difference between the line coming out of the nacelle and the center of the wind arrow).

•Show (right panel) on a relative scale, the incident wind (in blue), the wind due to the rotation of the blades (in black), and the total wind hitting the edge of the blade (in red).

•The blade angle (pitch)

•Graphically display the wind speed in relation to the nominal wind (blue arrow on the left panel).
