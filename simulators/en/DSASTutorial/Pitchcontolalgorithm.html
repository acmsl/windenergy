Blade Pitch Angle Control Algorithm

Wind Turbine Simulator with Active Stall and Two Speeds ›› Scope of the Simulation ›› Controller ›› Algorithms Used ››

The pitch angle of the blades can be common (all three with the same angle) or they can have different values. They are controlled by hydraulic cylinders or electric motors.

In the case of the wind turbine emulated in this simulator, the pitch of the three blades is common and is modified by hydraulic cylinders.

The Controller sends a signal (Controller.Control.PitchOut) that reaches the control input terminal of the servo control circuit in the nacelle that controls the hydraulic cylinders (Nacelle.DriveTrain.PitchIn).

The control algorithm used by the Controller takes into account the actual value of the pitch angle (Nacelle.DriveTrain.PitchNacelleOut) at any given moment through its input (Controller.Control.PitchNacelleIn) to implement a feedback control loop. This algorithm takes into account other factors, such as wind speed, to set the pitch angle value at all times.

The concepts taken into account are derived from those explained in Concepts on the extractable power of wind.

The pitch value changes to -90º (flag position) when disconnected from the grid and without production, so that mechanical stress on the blades, nacelle, and tower is minimized.
