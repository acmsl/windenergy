Inspection of gearboxes

What is a wind turbine? ›› Structure of a horizontal axis wind turbine ›› Gearboxes ››

The repair or replacement of a gearbox is a major maintenance operation, involving both direct costs (the repair or replacement itself) and indirect costs: rental of cranes and auxiliary equipment, logistical availability of spare parts, and lost profits due to the time the wind turbine is out of production.

For this reason, continuous gearbox monitoring equipment is now being installed: this equipment continuously measures parameters such as oil temperature, the amount of particles suspended in the oil, and, above all, the vibrations generated by the internal components. This equipment is known as "Preventive Maintenance" or "Condition Monitoring" (CMS).

The purpose of this equipment is to provide criteria for deciding when to inspect the gearboxes. These inspections are inexpensive (basically the loss of profit from taking the wind turbine out of production for a few hours) and are carried out using visual inspection with fiber optic equipment (videoscopes).
