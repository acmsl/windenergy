'Hydraulic Brake Circuit' Panel

The following figure shows the panel called 'Hydraulic Brake Circuit', which contains 5 channels.

1.The first 'channel' shows the pressure signal in the hydraulic circuit, as a percentage of the nominal value.

2. The second 'channel' shows the pump activation signal.

3. The third 'channel' shows the pressure switch output signal.

4. The fourth 'channel' displays two signals:

1. The 'Hard Brake' activation signal.

2. The 'Normal Brake' activation signal.

5. The fifth 'channel' displays the 'Brake released'signal.
