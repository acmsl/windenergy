Lubrication of gearboxes

What is a wind turbine? ›› Structure of a horizontal axis wind turbine ›› Gearboxes ››

The gearbox transmits all the mechanical power produced by the wind turbine, and therefore all its components (pinions, bearings, rolling surfaces, etc.) are subject to high mechanical stress and strain; for this reason, they must be properly lubricated. 

The oil used in lubrication prevents contact between the rolling and rolling surfaces in all rotating components. Therefore, an important part of the lubrication system is responsible for keeping the oil free of contaminants and suspended particles that could interfere and allow physical contact. Suspended particles can come from breakage and wear in sprockets, bearings, etc.

These systems also incorporate heat exchangers to maintain the oil temperature within appropriate ranges.
