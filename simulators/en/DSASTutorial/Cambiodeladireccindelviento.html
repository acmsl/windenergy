Changing the Orientation of the Nacelle

Wind Turbine Simulator with Active Stall and Two Speeds ›› Synoptics used in this simulator ›› Nacelle and Wind Orientation Synoptic (SynopticWindNacelle) ››

The nacelle direction changes in the following circumstances:

•When in "Production" mode, as a result of decisions made by the Controller related to excessive misalignment with respect to wind direction, or due to cable unwinding operations.

•When in "Stop" mode (see Stop button) using the "Manual Turn" buttons on the Control Terminal.

•It can be changed in any circumstance: using the "Left Turn" or "Right Turn" buttons on this synoptic.
These buttons usually show the status of the orientation motors, which correspond to signals coming from the Controller. However, they can be put into "command" mode using the following procedure:

oClick on the desired button with the left mouse button.

oPress the "F6" key: the border will turn red, indicating that the value of this button does not reflect the signal coming from the Controller.

oTry changing the status and you will see that the Gondola rotates in the indicated direction.

Note: if the Gondola's orientation is manually forced while in Production mode, exceeding the maximum number of turns of the power cables, the Controller will sound an alarm and stop the machine.
This procedure, used in "Stop" mode, is useful for setting the initial orientation value.
