Details of the power cables and associated sensors.

The generator is connected to the electrical grid by power cables, which "hang" from the nacelle (where the generator is located) to the base of the tower, where the transformer that raises the generator's voltage (690 V) to a medium voltage value (15KV / 22 KV), which is where the cable connecting it to the wind farm's electrical substation is connected.

As the nacelle has to be oriented towards the wind, its position is not fixed, so these internal power cables to the wind turbine twist as they descend from the nacelle to the base of the tower, depending on the direction of the wind. See figure below.


These cables have two fixed anchor points, one on each side of the nacelle base, which is the area where this twisting occurs. Logically, there is a maximum number of turns, which is typically 2.5 turns in each direction. 

There is no sensor for the number of turns of these cables, but the maximum limit is detected by a limit switch that is attached to the tower, and whose sensor end is tied to the end of the cable twist zone.

The controller uses the status of this limit switch to automatically stop the wind turbine and begin the process of unwinding the cables. See Algorithms for controlling the orientation and unwinding of power cables.
