Wind Turbine Simulator with Active Stall and Two Speeds

The "Two-Speed Wind Turbine Simulator" is an LdP application that simulates the behavior of a wind turbine with a squirrel cage electric generator, capable of operating at two speeds(corresponding to two generator synchronism speeds, corresponding to the two possible connections of the stator winding), and which has blade pitch control.

The power train control algorithm used is known as "Active Stalling" (i.e., active entry into aerodynamic losses), which is described in Stalling. The concept of "active" refers to the fact that the blade angle (pitch) is controlled in such a way that stalling occurs with a wind speed lower than that characteristic of the blade used. This method has the advantage that the range of angles that must be managed for control is much smaller than in the case of control by "letting the wind pass" ("to the feathers" in English), which implies greater simplicity of the pitch actuation mechanism.

Blade stalling is necessary to reduce the energy collected from the wind when it exceeds the nominal wind speed.

In addition to the functionalities related to:

•Operation (start-up, shutdown, nacelle orientation, pitch control, etc.)

•Safety (various shutdown incidents)

•the algorithm for changing the connection mode of the generator stator, i.e., the two possible operating speeds. This feature allows the wind turbine to better adapt to wind speeds below the nominal speed.

The LdP configuration for this simulator is called (for Spanish language) "DSASSpanish.conf", and defines a set of "Processes", "Synoptics", "Parameters" and "Connections."
