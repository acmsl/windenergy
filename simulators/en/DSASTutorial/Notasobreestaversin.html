What can you learn with this simulator?

Introduction ››

With this simulator, you will be able to:

•learn about the fundamentals and basic structure of a wind turbine.

•learn about its components, physical principles, and behavior.

•observe the evolution of internal variables when it enters and exits production.

•observe the reaction of its internal variables to changes in operating conditions, whether normal, fault, or exceptional.

This simulator is based on a type of wind turbine that was widely used until the early 2000s. It uses a squirrel cage generator and can change the angle of attack of the blades (pitch).

This tutorial is an integral part of the simulator and is organized into the following sections:

•What is a wind turbine?

In this section, you will find an introduction to the components of a typical wind turbine, along with the specific features of the "Active Stall" family.

•Physical principles

Here you will find a description of the fundamentals of the subsystems in a generic wind turbine and specific data relating to industrial wind turbines.

•Wind Turbine Simulator with Active Stall and Two Speeds:

This section describes the structure and components of this simulator. As it runs on the LdP platform, it can also be used as a test bench, where the parameters of the different subsystems can be changed to observe various behaviors. You will need to be familiar with the concepts of the LdP platform, which are covered in the "Introduction to LdP" tutorial.

•Practical Classes

This chapter contains guidelines for conducting a series of experiments with which you can reproduce situations that occur in real wind turbines, both under normal and exceptional conditions, and observe their effect on different internal variables, as well as study the influence of different parameters.

Each class contains a description of the steps to be taken with the simulator for your study.
