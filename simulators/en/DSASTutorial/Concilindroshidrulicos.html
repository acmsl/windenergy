In this case, the blades are attached to a gear ring associated with a motor. See the following figure.

 

These motors are controlled by servo systems located inside the hub, which respond to commands issued by the controller. Their action, in both directions, changes the angle of the corresponding blade.

These servo systems and motors are powered by uninterruptible power supplies, which must guarantee the energy necessary to move the blades to the "flag" position at any time. This is necessary to ensure that, even if the wind turbine loses connection to the power grid, it is possible to stop the blades from turning.
