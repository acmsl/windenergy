The following figure shows the states (squares) and events (arrows) of the State of Operation machine.

This programming is responsible for the following operations:

• connection and disconnection from the grid,

•unwinding of power cables,

• changing the connection type of the electric generator (4 or 6 poles)

The "Production" states are "Sg" and "SGG." The state of "operational but disconnected from the grid" is "St." The initial state is "SSTOPSM."

This machine is a slave to the Safety machine, i.e., it receives events from it. The Legend of the M.E. Operation Diagram lists the possible states and events.
