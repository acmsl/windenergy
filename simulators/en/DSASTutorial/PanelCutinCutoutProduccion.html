Yawing Panel

The following figure shows the panel called 'Yawing', which contains 2 channels.

1. Two signals are presented in the first 'channel':

1. Wind direction.
Signal "SynopticWindNacelle.WindDirectionOut." The value of this signal is between 0° and 360°.

2. The orientation of the nacelle
. Signal "Nacelle.Yawing.OrientationOut." The value of this signal can be between -900 and 900, to take into account the number of turns of the power cables.

2. The second 'channel' presents three digital signals corresponding to:

1. The limit switch activation signal associated with the power cables, which is activated when the absolute maximum number of turns has been reached in either direction.

2. The activation signal for the gondola's clockwise rotation motor (CW).

3. The activation signal for the gondola's counterclockwise rotation motor (CCW).
