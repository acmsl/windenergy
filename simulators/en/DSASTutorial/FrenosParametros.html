Brake system

Wind Turbine Simulator with Active Stall and Two Speeds ›› Scope of Simulation ›› Processes corresponding to Nacelle elements ›› Power Train ››

The brake system is simulated using the following criteria:

•Modification of pressure in the brake circuit according to:

oThe status of the pump.

oThe status of the relief valves.

oThe level of circuit losses.


The associated parameters are: 

Parameter

Use

NacelleBrake.PumpFlow

Determines the pump flow rate, as a percentage of the nominal pressure, per second.

NacelleBrake.LeakageBrakeHydrCircuit

Used to simulate total pressure losses due to hoses, hydraulic connection elements, pump leaks, etc. Measured in flow rate relative to nominal pressure per second.
