Example of state changes and events in the M.E. during a connection operation (cutin)

This simulator has a defined signal "Panel" (see Panel 'RPMs + Pitch + State Machines') that allows the evolution of two key variables (the generator shaft speed and the pitch angle) to be observed, together with the events and state changes of the two state machines.

The following figure shows the evolution of these signals during a grid connection operation (cutin).


The figures show the evolution of relevant signals (Controller.Pitch, Nacelle.Pitch, GeneratedPower, Thyristor, Bypass, Brake, GeneratorRPMs, Wind), the Events generated (STOPSeqEvent, OperationEvent) and the States (STOPSeqState, OperationState) for a run consisting of switching to Automatic mode (START) with low wind (5.2 m/s), making the cutin at that wind speed, changing the wind to about 9.0 m/s, disconnecting (momentary cutout to change generators connection) then cutin in a 9 m/s, then coming but to 4.6 m/s, disconnecting again and making again cutin at 4.6 m/s.
