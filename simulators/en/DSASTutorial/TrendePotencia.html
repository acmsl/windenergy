The blade (rotor) and generator RPM indicators are normally in "display only" mode, showing the corresponding data from the simulators included in the nacelle. However, they can be switched to "command" mode, in which case the value received by the controller can be modified by moving the slider or changing the value in the text.

The same applies to the "VCU" and "HCU" indicators.
