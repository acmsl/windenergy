Common synoptics of the LdP platform

Wind Turbine Simulator with Active Stall and Two Speeds ›› Synoptics used in this simulator ››

For a detailed description of the capabilities and basic synoptics of the LdP platform on which this simulator runs, please refer to the "Introduction to LdP" tutorial.

The common synoptics are accessible in the "Running Simulator xx" synoptic, third button from the left ("Synoptics") and refer to:

•the Signals defined in the different subsystems of the Simulator, and their connections ("Signals and Connections"),

•the parameters defined and associated with the different algorithms used, both for the simulation of physical elements and those used in the Controller. ("Parameters"),

•the creation of "Recorders" to visualize the evolution of signals.
