Electric Generator Requirements

The electric generator of a wind turbine is sized for its nominal power. In wind turbines of the type covered in this simulator, the generator is a short-circuited rotor (or squirrel cage) induction machine, and all the electrical power sent to the grid is obtained through the generator stator.

An introduction to the characteristics of these machines is provided in Introduction to Induction Machines.

The appendix Characteristics of the Generator Emulated in this Simulator presents the characteristics of a real model.

These types of generators are used in other facilities, such as cogeneration plants. They present the problem that the connection to the electrical grid must be made in such a way that it does not harm the facility or disturb the grid. This is usually achieved with a device called a soft connection to the grid.
