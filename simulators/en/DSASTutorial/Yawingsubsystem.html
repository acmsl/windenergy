Orientation System

Signals generated

In the "Nacelle" process, the following signals are created, corresponding to the elements in the nacelle that are related to the orientation of the wind turbine_

•Simulation of the two motor blocks that allow clockwise (CW) and counterclockwise (CCW) rotation.

•Simulation of the number of turns of the power cables that run from the nacelle to the base of the tower.

•Simulation of the limit switch that detects when the maximum number of turns has been reached.

•Generation of the wind vane signal, using the wind direction values (input to the system) and the orientation of the nacelle.

Algorithms used

•To generate the nacelle orientation signal, the following are taken into account:

oThe status of the input signals that govern the status of the yaw motors: SynopticWindNacelle.YawCCWOut, and SynopticWindNacelle.YawCWOut). These are normally signals that come from the Controller (Controller.Yawing.YCCWOut, and Controller.Yawing.YCWOut, respectively).

oThe yaw speed values of each of the motors that enter through the signals 'Nacelle.Yawing.CWYawingSpeed' and 'Nacelle.Yawing.CCWYawingSpeed' signals and are normally connected to the parameters "Parameter.NacelleYawing.CCWYawingSpeed" and "Parameter.NacelleYawing.CWYawingSpeed." In a real wind turbine, these values are in the order of 0.5 degrees/second. The recommended values, to enhance the simulation experience, are in the order of 10 degrees/second

The algorithm used integrates the time when both signals are active to calculate the current position of the nacelle orientation.

•To generate the number of turns of the power cables.

oBased on the orientation of the nacelle, counting the number of times it crosses the north direction.

•To generate the digital signal indicating that the maximum number of turns of the power cables has been reached.

oBy comparing the number of turns of the power cables with the value of the "Parameter.NacelleYawing.MaxCableTwist" parameter.

•To generate the wind vane signal.

Using the orientation of the nacelle and the direction of the wind.
