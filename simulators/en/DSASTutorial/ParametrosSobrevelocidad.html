Parameter

Use

Nacelle.GeneratorSpeedVCU

This value sets the maximum rotational speed that the electric generator shaft can reach (in rpm) before the specific emergency stop protocol is activated.

Nacelle.RotorSpeedforHCU

This value sets the maximum rotational speed that the blade shaft can reach (in rpm) before the specific emergency stop protocol is triggered.

This emergency stop is triggered if the one related to Nacelle.GeneratorSpeedVCU has failed and the power train continues to accelerate.
