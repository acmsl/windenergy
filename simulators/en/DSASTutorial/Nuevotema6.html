Design concepts for a planetary stage of a gearbox.


Herringbone gears

This type of gear has the following characteristics:

•It does not exert any lateral thrust on its support in either direction of rotation. Normal helical gears cause thrusts to appear on the rotation axis where they are placed, due to the thrust on each tooth. The direction of this thrust force depends on the direction of rotation of the gear. This is because each of the two parts of each tooth pushes in the opposite direction to the other. This eliminates the need for the bearings to withstand any lateral stress.

•Power transmission between gears is smoother than in the case of helical gears, because there is more contact surface between the teeth, and they "engage" more smoothly.

•They have a higher load capacity.

•However, they are more difficult to manufacture due to the additional complexity of the V-shape.
