Operator Control Terminal

Each wind turbine manufacturer provides a device known as a "Control Terminal" or "Control Terminal" intended to serve as the main tool for the Operator to perform operations related to the maintenance and tuning of the wind turbine.

These are generally portable devices capable of operating in harsh environments (rugged) in terms of temperature, humidity, etc.

They connect to the wind turbine controller via a specific communications port, usually ports with RS-485 protocol or similar.

They have a display, usually small, and a keyboard.

Functions

With the Control Terminal, the Operator can perform the following operations:

•Know exactly the status of the wind turbine, accessing all internal variables. Many of these variables are only accessible through the Control Terminal and not, for example, through the connection to the wind farm's SCADA system.

•Access historical incident data.

•Change parameters that influence the overall behavior of the wind turbine. To do this, the controller has multi-level "access control" mechanisms that allow different change operations depending on the operator's profile.
