Simulation of the Tower Vibration Sensor

Wind turbine simulator with active stall and two speeds ›› Synoptics used in this simulator ›› Power train synoptic (SynopticDriveTrain) ›› Common indicators ››

The tower has a vibration sensor. It is a 'pendulum' type, i.e., it gives a digital output that becomes active when the mass of the pendulum touches a ring that defines the maximum tolerated limit for oscillations.

This signal is simulated by the button labeled 'Tower Vibrations'.

The Controller only takes it into account when this signal remains 'active' for at least the time specified in this parameter. In the case of the simulator, this parameter is set to 2 seconds.

When this signal is activated and remains active for a certain time, the Controller initiates an immediate stop, even using the hydraulic brake.
