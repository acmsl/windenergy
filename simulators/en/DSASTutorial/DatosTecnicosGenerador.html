Generator characteristics

The generator used in the wind turbine on which this simulator is based has two ways of connecting the stator winding, offering the possibility of creating 2 pairs of poles (with a synchronous speed of 1500 rpm for 50 Hz) or 3 pairs of poles (with a synchronous speed of 1000 rpm).

The following sections show the data corresponding to these two connection possibilities.

rpm: revolutions per second.

I: effective current in the stator in amps

P: Active power (in kW)

Q: Reactive power (in KVA)

S: Total power (in KVA)

Power factor (Cos φ)

Mechanical torque: in N*m

The first table corresponds to the 4-pole magnetic connection and the second to the 6-pole connection.
