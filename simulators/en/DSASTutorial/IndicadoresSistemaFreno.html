Hydraulic brake circuit

 

Graphical indicators related to the brake circuit. They are in "presentation only" mode but can be switched to "command" mode (F6). See description of their interconnection in Indicator Connections


The hydraulic circuit is represented by a specific active graphic, which includes:

•The shoe that presses against the brake disc.

•The hydraulic actuator containing a spring to implement "Fail/Safe" operation

•A tank with the oil used in the hydraulic circuit.

•A hydraulic pump that maintains pressure in the circuit.

•A brake valve, which serves to quickly reduce the pressure in the circuit, so that the actuator spring forces the shoe against the brake disc and thus immobilizes it.

There is a set of graphic indicators that display the signals relating to this circuit. These indicators can be set to "command" mode (accessible with the "F6" key) to simulate faults.

•Pump status: comes from the Controller. When set to command mode, it directly controls the start/stop of the pump.

•The status of the two brake valves (the soft brake valve is not shown in the above diagram). These signals come from the Controller.

•The status of the hydraulic circuit pressure switch. This device has hysteresis (different limits depending on whether the pressure is rising or falling): these two limits define the green zone on the lower clock. This signal is input to the Controller.

•The "Brake circuit pressure" slider indicates the pressure as a percentage of the nominal value.

•The status of the limit switch that detects that the distance between the brake pad and the brake disc is sufficient for the brake not to be applied. This is indicated by the "Brake released" button. It is an input signal to the Controller.
