Control Terminal Synoptic

Wind Turbine Simulator with Active Stall and Two Speeds ›› Synoptics used in this simulator ››

This synoptic emulates the "Control Terminal" of a real wind turbine. It is used to start and stop the machine using the "START" and "STOP" buttons. It also allows manual control of the orientation ("Manual rotation").

It also displays information on key variables and parameters in the operation of the machine.

•About the wind: it provides the following information:

oThe actual wind speed, measured by the anemometer, in m/s.

oThe effective wind speed, due to the disorientation of the nacelle. When this disorientation is 0 degrees, the two values coincide.

oWind direction, in degrees.

oThe orientation of the nacelle, in degrees. If the number of cable turns is greater than 1, and you want to compare it with the wind direction, you need to compensate with 360 degrees per turn.

•Generator data: 

oRevolution speed at which it is operating.

oPower being generated.

oIts power factor.

•Power train data:

oBlade rotation speed.

oAcceleration.

oCurrent Cp value

oParameters being worked with:

▪Moment of inertia.

▪Air density.

•Requested blade angle (pitch) value.

•Generator connection type status: "Large Gen," "Small Gen."

•Status of thyristors and their bypass.

•Status of the orientation motors: "Left turn," "Right turn."

•Status of the hydraulic brake circuit pump.

•Whether there is voltage in each of the network phases, "F1 F2 F3."

•Status of the over-speed detectors on the low shaft of the multiplier (HCU) and the high shaft (VCU).

•The status of the "Vibration" detector in the Tower.

Additionally, this synoptic panel displays information on alarms, messages, etc., known in this simulator as "Incidents," both those that are "open" and those that have been recorded and moved to the "Incident history."
