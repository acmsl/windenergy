Start button

Wind Turbine Simulator with Active Stall and Two Speeds ›› Synoptics used in this simulator ›› Control Terminal Synoptic ››

The "START" button allows you to give the controller the command to begin the procedure for connecting to the grid and starting production, after first performing all the necessary checks.

To start this procedure, you must check the "Ok" column of the "Open Incidents" grid in the row corresponding to "Manual STOP."
