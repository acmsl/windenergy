What is a Wind Turbine? ››

It is important to always bear in mind that a wind turbine is an electricity generation plant designed to operate autonomously. In other words, it is a plant that does not require any human presence to operate. This means that it must have a control system that is properly programmed to take into account all incidents that may occur, such as

•external phenomena, such as hurricane-force winds, lightning strikes, excessive or very low temperatures, or

•incidents generated by the electrical grid to which it is connected (phase failures, line voltages outside limits, etc.), as well as 

•internal incidents, such as broken hydraulic components, sensor calibration errors or breakage, excessive vibrations, bearing problems, generator temperature rises, etc.

The controller is programmed to detect all these incidents in a timely manner and to respond to them. This means that the way to interact with a wind turbine is by communicating with its controller, which is done through specific data terminals.
