Active copies of the Logger

The concept of "active copy" provides "snapshots" of the signal evolution graph of a recorder at a given moment and also allows time measurements to be taken on them.

Indication of the moment associated with the cursor

When the mouse is over the synoptic, the moment corresponding to that position is indicated in "Current."

Clicking the right mouse button when hovering over this synoptic allows you to set the moment that will serve as the origin of time measurements. This action is marked with a yellow line. From that moment on, when you move the mouse over the synoptic, a blue vertical line appears, which follows it, while indicating the distance in time in the indicator titled "Delta time."

Measuring the time distance between two events

When the "Time reference" has been set, a yellow vertical line appears in all the "Recorders," and the upper area shows two time values: "Ref," which indicates the reference moment, and "Delta time." The latter indicator shows the time distance (in milliseconds) between the "Time reference" and the current moment where the mouse cursor is located (blue line).
