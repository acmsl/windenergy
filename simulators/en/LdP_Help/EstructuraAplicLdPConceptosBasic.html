Basic concepts

Structure of LdP applications ››


The structure of LdP is designed to facilitate the design of simulators: a basic concept is to use a very intuitive and accepted paradigm: an electrical cabinet or electronic device. These devices have independent and interconnected "components" (electrical or electronic). The connections between these components are made using electrical cables that connect "output signals" with "input signals." An output signal can be connected to one or more input signals, but not to another output signal.

As a result, these connections can be represented with an electrical diagram.

LdP has "tools" for observing these connections and changing them, so that their functionalities match those of a "workbench or laboratory," where power supplies, voltmeters, ammeters, oscilloscopes, etc. are available.

In addition, LdP provides a database record of all changes that occur in the Simulator signals, with a resolution of 1 millisecond.
