Introduction to Wind Turbine Experiments

🌬️ Welcome to the Virtual Wind Turbine Laboratory

This manual will guide you through a series of practical experiments designed to understand the operation of different wind turbine technologies through virtual simulation.

What will you find in this manual?

This document contains a detailed description of practical experiments that you can carry out using the Wind Farm Simulator suite of simulators. These simulators work as Virtual Work Benches that allow you to experiment with different types of wind turbines without the need for physical equipment.

💻 About Wind Farm Simulator

Wind Farm Simulator is a suite of educational simulators that reproduces the behaviour of different families of wind turbines under realistic conditions. It allows you to:

  • Observe system behaviour in real time
  • Modify operational and configuration parameters
  • Take measurements and make comparisons
  • Experiment with different wind conditions
  • Analyse the performance of different technologies

Manual structure

Each experiment in this manual includes:

📋 Components of each experiment:

  • Learning objectives: What you will learn upon completing the experiment
  • Theoretical background: Concepts needed to understand the experiment
  • Step-by-step procedure: Detailed instructions for carrying out the experiment
  • Associated tutorials: Links to in-depth supplementary material
  • Analysis activities: Tasks to consolidate learning

Learning methodology

This manual is designed following an active learning methodology. Do not limit yourself to reading: the aim is that you experiment, observe, analyse and compare the results obtained.

🎯 Pedagogical approach

The experiments are organised so that you develop a progressive understanding:

  1. Understanding basic principles: How and why wind turbines work
  2. Comparative analysis: Differences between different technologies
  3. Operation and control: Yaw, pitch and regulation systems
  4. Dynamic response: Behaviour under changing wind conditions
  5. Economic aspects: Operating costs and energy efficiency

How to use this manual

📖 Recommendations to make the most of it:

  • Follow the proposed order: The experiments are didactically sequenced
  • Read the tutorials: Before each experiment, review the indicated tutorials
  • Take notes: Record your observations and results
  • Experiment freely: Once an experiment is completed, modify parameters and observe the effects
  • Compare technologies: Many experiments will ask you to compare different types of wind turbines

Wind turbine families you will study

Throughout the experiments you will work with three main families of wind turbines:

⚡ Wind turbine technologies:

  1. DSAS (Direct Stall Active Stall): Squirrel cage induction generator directly connected to the grid, with active stall control
  2. RRC (Rotor Resistance Control): Wound rotor induction generator with rotor resistance control
  3. DFIG (Doubly Fed Induction Generator): Doubly fed induction generator with back-to-back converters

Prerequisites

To make the most of these experiments, it is recommended that you have basic knowledge of:

  • Physics (mechanics, electricity)
  • Rotating electrical machines
  • Basic concepts of wind energy
