Practical Labs

Introduction to the Practical Labs

Welcome to the hands-on part of your wind turbine education!

Now that you understand the theory, it's time to see everything in action. These practical labs use the wind turbine simulator to let you:

  • Observe how the turbine operates in different conditions
  • Experiment with parameters safely (no real equipment at risk!)
  • Understand the control logic by watching it work
  • Troubleshoot faults and learn how the system responds
  • Build intuition that would take years to develop in the field

What Makes These Labs Valuable:

In the real world:

  • ✗ Can't experiment freely (too expensive, too dangerous)
  • ✗ Can't see inside the Controller's "mind"
  • ✗ Can't force failure conditions safely
  • ✗ Must wait for conditions to occur naturally
  • ✗ Limited time on actual turbines

In the simulator:

  • ✓ Experiment freely without consequences
  • ✓ See all internal states and signals
  • ✓ Force any condition you want to study
  • ✓ Control time and conditions
  • ✓ Unlimited practice time

How to Use These Labs:

Each lab follows this structure:

  1. Objective: What you'll learn
  2. Preparation: How to set up the simulator
  3. Procedure: Step-by-step instructions
  4. Observations: What to watch for
  5. Analysis: Understanding what happened
  6. Experiments: Try variations on your own

Important Notes:

  • Take your time! There's no rush
  • Don't just follow steps mechanically - think about WHY things happen
  • Make notes of interesting observations
  • Try the suggested experiments
  • Come up with your own experiments!
