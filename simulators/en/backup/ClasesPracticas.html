Laboratory Sessions

The following chapters present a series of laboratory sessions designed to help you explore and understand the behavior of the wind turbine under a variety of operating conditions. Each session focuses on a specific aspect of turbine performance and provides guided exercises to support hands‑on learning.

The set of sessions included here is not exhaustive. Additional laboratory activities may be developed to introduce realistic operational scenarios that require the student to diagnose and resolve issues within the wind turbine. These extended cases typically involve creating alternative configurations that differ from those used in this simulator. Such differences do not affect the core structure of the configuration but may involve adjustments to parameter sets, signal routing, or other implementation details. For guidance on developing new configurations, please contact ACM SL.
