Summary of the Algorithms Implemented in the Wind Turbine Controller

The wind turbine controller incorporates several key algorithms that govern its operation under a wide range of conditions. These algorithms ensure safe, efficient, and reliable performance by coordinating the main subsystems of the turbine and responding to both environmental inputs and operational requirements.

The implemented algorithms include:
  • Blade pitch control — Regulates the blade angle to adapt to changing wind conditions and to manage the turbine’s operating states, including start‑up (cut‑in), shutdown (cut‑out), and safety stop procedures.
  • Hydraulic brake pressure control — Manages the hydraulic circuit by regulating the pump and brake valves to ensure proper braking performance during normal and emergency operations.
  • Nacelle yaw control — Adjusts the nacelle orientation by commanding the yaw motors to align the rotor with the wind direction.
  • Power cable unwinding control — Prevents excessive twisting of the power cable by monitoring nacelle rotation and commanding the yaw motors to unwind the cable when necessary. See power cable unwinding for more details.
  • Grid monitoring — Continuously supervises electrical grid parameters to ensure that the turbine operates within acceptable limits and complies with grid‑connection requirements.
  • Safety event monitoring — Detects and evaluates operational events that may compromise turbine safety, triggering protective actions when required.
