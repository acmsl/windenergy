Introduction

The "Dual Speed Active Stall Wind Turbine Simulator" is one of the © ACM SL family of Simulators, specific for Wind Turbines, that use squirrel cage electric generators, dual generators or a generator with two winding connections, and the Active Stall control method for Blades Pitch. It consists of a configuration file for the LdPv10 application, ("DSASWTSimulator.conf") and associated components.

It is a training system specialized for actual wind turbines, designed for training personnel that maintain and operate Wind Turbines in a Wind Farm, as well as a training system to introduce the concepts implied in the management of a Wind Farm from the point of view of production.

Using it, the trainee can exercise all the combinations of commands, access the statuses of Wind Turbines internal subsystems and the installation's parameters that they may encounter in a real Wind Turbine.

Its design allows very close to reality hands-on experience on how to operate typical Wind Turbines that are common nowadays in Wind Farms. 

For example, the trainee can observe what is the  planned reaction  for extreme winds. Or how  the system solves the loss of efficiency of the braking system. Or the influence of  higher than expected temperature in the gearbox or in the generator.

To achieve this goal we present in this tutorial all the physics principles governing the various subsystems implied in the design of real Wind Turbine in the field.

A "Dual Speed Active Stall Wind Turbine"  has three Blades facing the wind, mounted on an horizontal axis (HAWT), operates on the stall principle (see here for more information), with dual speed (to adapt with efficiency to a wider range of wind speeds), with a asynchronous generator connected directly to the Grid. 

The family of © ACM SL Wind Energy simulators includes other more sophisticated models, such as the Rotor Resistor Controlled Wind Turbine and the Doubly Fed Induction Generator Wind Turbine (DFIG): we strongly recommend to start with the "Dual Speed Active Stall Wind Turbine Simulator".

In this simulator, we concentrate  on understanding, and be acquainted with, the capabilities and reactions of a variable pitch Wind Turbine with asynchronous generator connected directly to the Grid, and with capabilities to adapt to different wind regimes using only a dual speed generator. Other designs reduce the complexity of the mechanical principles through the use of advanced power electronics energy conversion systems. Those solutions, more expensive but more versatile, solve the problem of adapting to different wind speeds to the energy conversion system. 

This simulator include a number of simulators for each of the subsystems in the Nacelle. So the trainee can change, say, the moment of inertia of the DriveTrain, and see the influence in Wind Turbines evolution for the operations of entering production and stopping production.

The purposes of this simulator, therefore, are:

•To emulate the behavior of every subsystem inside a Wind Turbine, so the observed behavior is very close to the real machine.

•Present a set of visualization tools so the user can effectively watch the values of every single signal inside the Wind Turbine, and see its evolution.

•To interact with the Wind Turbines changing the environment conditions (wind speed, wind direction, Grid connection, temperatures, etc) in order to exercise all their possible operations.

•To understand the security aspects of operation of the Wind Turbines.

•To easily set uncommon situations of the external conditions (extreme winds, for example) and on conditions inside the Wind Turbine itself, such as breaking an electrical connection, or forcing vibrations on the Tower, etc.

As a consequence the user of this simulator may:

•Understand the physical principles on which a Wind Turbine is based.

•Know how is the internal structure of a Wind Turbine.

•Know in detail the algorithms used in every subsystem and the parameters used in them.

•Understand the design principles for  the controller of a Wind Turbine.
