Changing the wind direction

Teaching sessions ››

The Wind Turbine must be kept upwind for production in order to take the maximum energy from the wind. That is achieved rotating the Nacelle around the vertical axis of the pole, using the yawing motors (see Yawing subsystem). It is common to have two motors, one for each rotating direction (ClockWise -CW- or CounterClockWise-CCW).


The Controller has an algorithm that commands these two motors. The inputs to this algorithm are:

1.the Nacelle orientation, normally measured taking the North as reference an increasing clockwise.

2.the wind direction.

You can use the "WindSpeed"  widget in the "SynopticWindNacelle" synoptic for changing the wind direction and observe how the Wind Turbine follows that change (automatic mode) or you can use the "yawing buttons" in the "Control Terminal" for turning them on manually: you have to be in "Manual" mode.
