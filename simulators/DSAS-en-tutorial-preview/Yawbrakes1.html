In the above figure can be appreciated one of the Yaw motors, the Yaw ring, fixed to the Tower and allows the turning of the Nacelle when the Yaw motors are activated. The Yaw pads, electrically actuated, fix the position of the Nacelle when it gets aligned with the wind direction.
