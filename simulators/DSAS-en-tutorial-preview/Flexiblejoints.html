GearBoxes

The purpose of the Gearbox is to transmit the mechanical torque created by the Blades connected to the Hub to the mechanical axis of the electric Generator, adapting it to the speed required by the electric Generator.

Next figure shows the cross section of a typical Gearbox that has 3 stages, one of them with planetary gears.

A simplified schema is the following

Schematic of three stages Gearbox with a planetary input (Source AeroTorque Inc.)

The working mode is:

•Hub is connected to the Planet carrier (4) (blue) through the Rotor Shaft (not shown). This Planet carrier is attached to the planets (shaded yellow areas).

•The satellites are engaged with the inside of the support of this stage (shaded green), which in turn is fixed to the Gearbox's case, thus fixed. The satellites, forced by the input axis, and engaged also with the Sun pinion(central shaded red area), move internal axis labeled (3).

•The other two stages are normal and move the axis labeled (2) and (1).

•Grey crossed areas corresponds to bearings.

One of the main advantages of Planetary stage is that the contact surface with the Sun pinion is multiplied by the number of planets, thus reducing their stress.    

The speed ratio between its two axis varies, depending on the range of rpms of the Hub (that depends on the Blades length and wind speed regimes they must work on), and the working speed of the Generator.

Typically it ranges between 1:50 for small machines (600 KW where Generator rpms may be 1500 rpms -for 50 Hz to 1800 rpms -for 60 Hz) to 1:90 for big machines.
