Behavior facing very high wind speeds

Teaching sessions ››

In wind limits you may find a description of the wind speed values for which the Wind Turbine is operational.

Clearly when the wind speed is very high the Wind Turbine should be kept in the safest position (Blades to -90º, brakes on).  The limit for that wind speed is reflected in the parameter WindAbsolutMax - typically 30 m/s.

For standard operation there is an upper limit (reflected in the parameter WindMaxForOperation -typically 25 m/s-).

There may be cases for wind conditions in which wind speed gets above WindMaxForOperation but below WindAbsolutMax: this is a typical case for high wind gusts. Depending on the strength of the mechanical design of the Wind Turbine (Blades + pole), and if the Wind Turbine is in production, it is advisable not to stop the Wind Turbine (stop production) unless the time for that situation last longer than a given amount of time. In the simulator this value of wind is associated to the parameter WindMaxForTemporalOperation -typically 28 m/s-, and the time to wait in this case is fixed to 100 seconds.
