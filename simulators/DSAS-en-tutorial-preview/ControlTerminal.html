Control Terminal

Description of the Simulator ›› Emulated Wind Turbine ››

Each manufacturer of Wind Turbines provide a device known as "Control Terminal" that the Operator must use to access the Controller of the Wind Turbine.

There are several types, from special purpose rugged terminals with only the basic display and keyboard facilities to applications running in rugged PCs.

Functions

The Control Terminal is the basic tool used by the Opertors in order to:

•Know the exact situation of the Wind Turbine.

•Access to internal variables of the Wind Turbine, including many that are not accessible via other means, such as the SCADA connection.

•Access to historical data (production, energy, incidences, etc).

•Access to settings, basically parameters, that are used to fine tune the behavior of the WIndTurbine to an specific site.

The Control Terminal is also used by the internal application in the Controller of the Wind Turbine to force the acknowledgment of some types of incidences. In this way the Controller can maintain a log of all the incidences that happen to the Wind Turbine, when they were reported and when they were acknowledged.
