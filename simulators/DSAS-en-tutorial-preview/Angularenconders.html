Incremental encoders

You have a presentation on how these encoders are made and works here.

They contain two sets of circular slots, with their corresponding lighting and sensing elements: A  and B.


The absolute position of the shaft can be obtain using an external counter that receives the pulse signal from A and B.

Another approach is to use a single slot and placing the two sensor "90º apart". See next figure.

The relationship between the edges (trailing and falling) of these two pulse signals (which of the two changes first) defines the sense of rotation. See next figure.


These encoders can be used for measuring the rotational speed, counting the number of edges per second using an external counter.
