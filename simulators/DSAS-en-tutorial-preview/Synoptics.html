Synoptics

Description of the Simulator ››

Synoptics constitute the User Interface of the Simulators. The provide access to the internal variables used in the simulators included in a given configuration and also present them in a adhoc manner (with linear or circular graphic widgets, in plots updated in real-time, in striprecorders, etc).

In every LdP configuration there are two sets of synoptics: 

a) The set of synoptics for controlling the execution of the simulation, and exploring and setting parameters for the sub-simulators and their connectivity, etc. These are known as "Common synoptics for LdP".

b) The specific synoptics associated to the sub-simulators used in this particular configuration.
