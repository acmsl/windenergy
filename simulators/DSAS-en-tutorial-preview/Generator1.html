Generator

The Generator is characterized by real data from a actual electrical generator used in one of these Wind Turbines. You can find these characteristics in Generator's characteristics.

These values are used in the ControlEmulator in order to implement the behavior of the Wind Turbine on the different phases: cutin, cutout and production.

The temperature of the generator is monitored by the ControlEmulator, so when it reach a certain level (set by parameter Controller.GeneratorFanEntryTemp) it turns on the fan. If it reaches another level (set by parameter Controller.GeneratorMaxTemp) it stops the Wind Turbines.
