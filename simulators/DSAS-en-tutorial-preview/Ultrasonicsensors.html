Laser based sensors (Lidar)

What is a Wind Turbine ›› Structure of a Wind Turbine ›› Meteorological sensors ›› Combined sensors ››

The Lidar is based on measurements using the Doppler effect of signals emitted by a laser located either in the Meteorological Mast of the Wind Farm, or in the Wind Turbine itself.

The interface of these devices is through common communications buses (RS485, CANBus, Profibus, etc.)

These devices are able to map wind speed and direction, as well as turbulence and wind shear well above the tip of the blades ( 200 m.)

A good reference is from NREL: "LIDAR Wind Speed Measurements of Evolving Wind Fields".
