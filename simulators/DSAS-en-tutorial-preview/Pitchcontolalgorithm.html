Pitch control algorithm

Description of the Simulator ›› Emulated Wind Turbine ›› Controller ›› Algorithms ››

The pitch of the Blades is controlled via a hydraulic or electrical actuator located in the Nacelle. This actuator receives a command signal from the Controller. This actuator replicates the commanded value to the three Blades. There are procedures to counteract the possible differences among the three Blades. Those procedures involve mechanical and electronic adjustments. The service personnel can set the Wind Turbine to specific states where those adjustments can be ready made.

The policies that the Controller uses for setting the value of the pitch depends on the state and situations. Conceptually, as explained in Physics of a Wind Turbine, the pitch controls the forces that permits the acceleration or deceleration of the rotation of the Hub with the Blades.

Acceleration occurs when the Wind Turbine tries to reach the connection with the Grid (cutin). The conditions are to arrive to that situation in the fastest way but taking into consideration two aspects: a) the stress supported by the whole DriveTrain and, specially, the Blades, and b) that the acceleration should not overcome a limit fixed by the generator imantation process.

Deceleration occurs when the Controller wants to stop the whole DriveTrain. There is a lot of kinetic energy stored in the DriveTrain when the generator is disconnected from the Grid (cutout). This energy is passed back to the air forcing the Blades to an angle that oppose its turning around the Hub shaft. In order to understand how this happen you have to look at the Cp (aerodynamic coefficient) of the blade, a value to which the torque produced by the wind is proportional.
