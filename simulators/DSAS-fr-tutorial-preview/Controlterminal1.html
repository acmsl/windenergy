Synoptique 'Terminal de Contrôle'.

Description du Simulateur ›› Synoptiques ››

Le synoptique appelé "Terminal de Contrôle"" a deux objectifs: d'une part émuler le dispositif physique appelé "Control Terminal" (voir ici et ici) ,et d'autre part servir d'interface graphique utilisateur pour accéder aux fonctionnalités associées au "ControllerEmulator"


This synoptic has several parts designed to present as much information as possible to the user.

It is used to present to the user the following information:

•Outputs:

oThe value of the command pitch for the Blades.

oThe status and mode of the braking system, including the status of the pump and if brake is released.

oThe status of the yawing motors

oThe type of connection of the Generator (Large:4 poles, Small: 6 poles) 

oThe conduction status of the Thyristors

oThe status of the thyristor's Bypass.

•Inputs:

oFrom the Nacelle:

▪The state of the brakes (released or not)

▪The actual state of the connection of the Generator.

▪The Nacelle orientation

▪The value of the detectors of over-speed (HCU and VCU)

▪The status of the Pole Vibrations

▪Wind direction and speed

▪Actual Rotor and Generator RPMs

▪Actual Generated Power and its Power Factor

oFrom the Grid:

▪The status of each Grid phase

oParameters:

▪Moment of inertia of the DriveTrain

▪Air density.

•Values for internal variables used in the algorithms:

oAerodynamic coefficient (Cp)

oActual acceleration of the DriveTrain

oEffective wind speed (wind speed perpendicular to the blade's rotation plane).

•Buttons for the Manual Control of the Yawing

•Buttons for activating the "normal" operation ("START") or passing to Manual control ("STOP")

•Information about the internal State Machines (Operation and Security):

oActual State

oLast received Event
