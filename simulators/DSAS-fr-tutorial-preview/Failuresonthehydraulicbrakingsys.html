Other sections of this document explain the functionality of the braking system; here we are interested in showing that, unlike a car, the braking system is not the usual way of stopping the DriveTrain. To minimize the wear of brake pads, the air is normally used to stop, setting the blade's pitch to a low value, so that the resulting forces or the air on the Blades result in a deceleration of the driver train speed. 

On the other hand, the braking system is critical for operational safety and system maintenance. It is also the mechanism to solve overspeed situations (HCU and VCU issues) as discussed in the corresponding section. 

That is why the Controller uses a series of signals to verify that the hydraulic system is kept in working conditions.
